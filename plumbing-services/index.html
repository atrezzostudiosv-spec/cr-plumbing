02 / Plumbing

Plumbing Repairs and Installs for DFW Homes

North Texas is hard on plumbing. The clay under most DFW homes swells when it rains and shrinks in August, and that movement stresses slab pipes and sewer lines. Add hard water and the odd hard freeze, and you’ve got the three things behind most of the calls we get.

So most of our work is fixing what that does. We repair leaking faucets, running toilets and failed garbage disposals. We hook up dishwashers, ice makers and washers so they don’t leak behind the cabinet. When a drain keeps clogging, we’ll camera it first and tell you whether hydro jetting or a trenchless sewer repair actually makes sense. No digging up the yard unless it’s the only way.

We also handle the work that protects the house. That includes backflow prevention and testing, sump pump installs for low lots and crawl spaces, and water line repair when your pressure drops or the bill jumps.

Water heaters are a big part of what we do. Hard water shortens their life here, so we see a lot of failing tanks. We repair them when that’s worth it and replace them when it isn’t, with either a tank water heater or a tankless unit. We’ll tell you which fits your house and how you use hot water.

03 / Plumbing

Every Plumbing Service We Offer

Most people find a plumber the hard way, mid-leak and in a hurry. That’s a bad time to be guessing. We’d rather you know how we work up front: we show up when we say we will, explain what’s wrong in plain English, and give you a price before anything gets fixed. We’re based in Argyle and work across Denton and Tarrant counties, from Flower Mound and Southlake to Fort Worth and North Richland Hills. See the full list on our service areas page.

A burst pipe can put water under your floors in minutes. Shut off the main valve first; it’s usually near the street or where the line enters the house. Then call us at (940) 370-5663. We’ll find the source, stop the leak and tell you what it’ll take to fix it properly.

If you’ve got a low lot, a crawl space or a yard that holds water after a storm, a sump pump earns its keep. We install new pumps, swap out tired ones and add battery backups. The backup matters more than people think. Spring storms here tend to knock the power out at exactly the wrong moment.

Sump Pump Installation and Repair

A bad dishwasher or ice maker connection rarely fails loudly. It drips behind the cabinet for months until the floor goes soft. We connect dishwashers, washing machines, ice makers and fridge lines with the right shutoffs and fittings, then check for leaks before we leave.

Appliance plumbing and hookups

Backflow is when dirty water gets pulled back into your clean supply. Irrigation systems are the usual culprit. A backflow preventer stops it, and many DFW cities require a yearly test. We install, repair and test backflow devices for homes and irrigation systems.

Backflow prevention and testing

A running toilet can waste hundreds of gallons a day without you noticing. Same goes for a slow faucet drip. We fix leaking faucets, toilets, shower valves and supply lines. When it’s time for something new, we install faucets, sinks, toilets and showerheads, and help you pick ones that use less water.

Faucet and toilet repair · Fixture installation

If you’ve snaked the same drain three times this year, the clog isn’t the problem. The buildup lining the pipe is. Hydro jetting blasts it off with high-pressure water: grease, scale, sludge and even small roots. A snake only pokes a hole through. We camera the line first to make sure the pipe’s sound enough to jet.

Hydro jetting · Drain cleaning

Gurgling drains, a sewage smell in the yard or a backup in the lowest tub are the usual warning signs. Here, the cause is often tree roots or a pipe that’s shifted with the soil. We run a camera down the line so you can see the damage yourself. Then we lay out the options, including trenchless repair that leaves your lawn and driveway alone.

Sewer line services · Trenchless sewer repair

A spike in your water bill with no obvious leak usually means something’s wrong underground or under the slab. We use leak detection equipment to pinpoint it without tearing up more than we need to. Then we repair the section or replace the line, whichever holds up better long term.

Water line repair · Leak detection

Most tank water heaters last 8 to 12 years. Hard water can cut that short. Rumbling, rusty water or a slow drip from the base are signs yours is near the end. We repair units that are worth saving and replace the ones that aren’t, with a new tank or a tankless system that heats on demand.

Water heater services · Tankless water heaters

DFW tap water is safe to drink, but it’s hard and heavily treated. You’ll see it as white crust on faucets, spotty dishes and dry skin. A whole-home filter takes out chlorine and other contaminants. A softener deals with the minerals that build up in pipes, water heaters and appliances. We can test your water and tell you which one you need, or if you need both.

Questions / Answers

GOOD TO
KNOW.

How much does a plumber cost in DFW?

It depends on the job, so we don’t quote blind over the phone. A plumber looks at the problem, then gives you a set price before starting. You can say yes or no. For bigger jobs like a water heater or sewer line, ask about our financing options.

Can you come out today?

Often, yes. Call early and we’ll give you a real arrival window, not a vague “sometime this afternoon.”

Do you install sump pumps and repair them too?

Both. We fix pumps that are worth fixing and replace the rest. If yours doesn’t have a battery backup, we’ll usually suggest adding one.

Why do I need a backflow preventer?

It keeps irrigation water, fertilizer and anything else on the dirty side from flowing back into the water you drink. Many cities require one on sprinkler systems, plus a test every year.

Is it worth repairing my water heater, or should I replace it?

If it’s under about 8 years old and the tank isn’t leaking, a repair usually makes sense. A thermostat, element or valve is a quick fix. Older, rusty or leaking tanks are better replaced. We’ll give you both prices so you can compare.

My drain keeps clogging. Is that a bigger problem?

It can be. A drain that clogs again within weeks usually has buildup or roots further down the line. A camera inspection shows us exactly what’s there before we pick a fix.

Do you fix all types of plumbing fixtures?

Yes. That covers faucets, toilets, sinks, tubs, shower valves and the supply lines that feed them. We work on most major brands.

Are your plumbers licensed?

Yes. Our plumbers are licensed in Texas, and everyone we send to your home is background-checked.
