Hydrojet Drain Cleaning Services in Texas

Hydrojetting is the most thorough way to clear a blocked drain or sewer line. A high-pressure hose delivers water at up to 4,000 PSI through a specialised nozzle, scouring the inside walls of the pipe and flushing everything out. It handles grease, soap scum, sludge, mineral scale and root intrusion — the things that build up slowly and cause the same drain to clog again and again.

Every hydrojet drain cleaning job starts with a camera inspection. We run a scope down the line to find the blockage, confirm what it is, and check the condition of the pipe itself. That last part matters: hydrojetting is safe for pipes in sound condition, but a line that is already cracked or collapsing needs repair, not pressure. Inspecting first is how we avoid making a bad pipe worse.

Once the line is clear, we re-scope it so you can see the result. If the inspection turns up a deeper problem — a bellied line, root damage, a break — we will tell you and walk you through the repair options, including trenchless sewer repair where it fits.

Hydrojetting vs Snaking: Which Does Your Drain Need?

A cable auger, or snake, works by boring a hole through the blockage. Water starts flowing again, so the drain seems fixed — but the grease, sludge or roots are still coating the pipe wall, and the opening closes back up over the following weeks or months.

Hydrojetting scours the full diameter of the pipe. Nothing is left behind for the next clog to build on. That is why hydrojetting costs more up front and why it is the right call when a drain has been snaked before and clogged again.

Snaking is still the better choice for a simple, one-off blockage close to the fixture, and for pipes in poor structural condition that cannot take the pressure. The camera inspection tells us which situation you are in before we recommend either one.

Hydrojetting costs more than snaking a drain, and less than repeatedly snaking the same drain every few months. The price depends on which line needs clearing, how accessible the cleanout is, and what the camera inspection shows.

A single kitchen or bathroom line is the lower end. A full main sewer line jetting job, with camera inspection before and after, sits higher. Commercial and restaurant work is quoted on the property.

Questions / Answers

GOOD TO
KNOW.

How do I know if I need hydrojetting?

If a drain has been snaked before and clogged again, hydrojetting is usually the answer. Other signs: multiple fixtures backing up at once, slow drains throughout the house, gurgling sounds, or sewage smell around floor drains. A camera inspection confirms it.

Does hydrojetting damage old pipes?

Not when it is done correctly. Hydrojetting is safe for pipes in sound structural condition, and our technicians adjust pressure and nozzle to the pipe material. This is exactly why we run a camera inspection first — a line that is already cracked or collapsing needs repair, not pressure, and we will tell you before we jet.

How long does the hydrojetting take?

Most residential drain jetting jobs are completed in a couple of hours including the camera inspection before and after. A full main sewer line, or a heavily blocked commercial line, can take longer.

Is hydrojetting different from snaking a drain?

Yes. A snake bores a hole through the blockage so water flows again, but leaves the buildup on the pipe walls. Hydrojetting scours the full pipe diameter and flushes everything out. That is why snaked drains often re-clog within months and jetted ones do not.

Can I use this service in restaurants or commercial spaces?

Yes, and commercial kitchens are one of the best uses for it. Grease volume in a restaurant line is far higher than in a home, and scheduled hydrojetting prevents the mid-service backup that shuts a kitchen down. We service restaurants and commercial properties across DFW.

What’s the cost of hydrojetting?

It depends on which line needs clearing, how accessible the cleanout is, and what the camera inspection shows. A single kitchen or bathroom line is at the lower end; a full main sewer line with inspection sits higher. We quote the price before starting work.

Do you jet sewer lines?

Yes. Sewer line hydro jetting is one of our main services. We access the line at the cleanout, scope it to find the blockage, jet the full run, and re-scope to confirm it is clear. This handles roots, sludge and scale that a cable auger cannot remove.

How often should hydrojetting be done?

For most homes, only when there is a problem. For properties with recurring blockages or commercial kitchens, a scheduled interval makes sense — often annually or semi-annually. We base the recommendation on what the camera inspection shows rather than a generic schedule.

Will hydrojetting improve overall plumbing performance?

Yes. Clearing the full pipe diameter restores the line to its designed capacity, so drains empty faster and the system handles normal use without backing up. It also removes the buildup that future clogs would form on.
