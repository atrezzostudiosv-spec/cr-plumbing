02 / Plumbing

OUR APPLIANCE PLUMBING SERVICES

The new dishwasher is sitting in the garage, the old one’s already out, and the install looks simple enough. Then the old supply line turns out to be too short, the shutoff under the sink won’t close all the way, and the drain fitting doesn’t match the disposal. That’s usually when people call us.

We take care of the water and drain side of appliance installs. Dishwasher installation and hookup, including the supply line, drain hose and connection to your sink drain or disposal. Washing machine hookups, from swapping a leaky washer box to fixing a drain that overflows mid-cycle. Refrigerator and ice maker water lines, run from the nearest cold line to the back of the fridge with its own shutoff.

Hooking up a gas stove or dryer? That involves the gas line, so it has its own page: see our gas appliance hookups.

01

Pressure control

Water pressure that runs high is hard on appliance valves and hoses. We check yours and can add a pressure-reducing valve if the house needs one.

02

Leak testing

We run the appliance and check every fitting before we leave. A small drip is easy to miss until it’s soaked the cabinet floor.

03

Ice Maker & Fridge Water Lines

No water line behind the fridge? We’ll run one from the nearest cold supply and add a shutoff, so the ice maker and water dispenser work without drips.

04

Water line installation

New hot and cold supply lines to the appliance, sized correctly, with braided stainless connectors instead of the old rubber hoses that split with age.

05

Shutoff valve setup

A dedicated shutoff for each appliance, somewhere you can actually reach it. If the old valve has seized, we replace it while we’re there.

06

Drain connections

Dishwasher drains tied into the sink or disposal with a proper high loop, and washing machine drains set at the right height so they don’t siphon or overflow.

Questions / Answers

GOOD TO
KNOW.

What appliances do you install and connect?

Dishwashers, washing machines, and refrigerators with ice makers or water dispensers. For gas stoves, dryers and other gas appliances, see our gas appliance page.

Why is professional appliance plumbing important?

Most appliance water damage starts at the connection, not the appliance. A reused supply line, a drain hose with no high loop, a shutoff that won’t close. None of it is hard to get right. It just has to be done carefully.

Can improper installation void my appliance warranty?

It can. Most manufacturers list installation requirements in the manual, and damage from a connection that doesn’t meet them may not be covered. We follow those instructions on every install.

What does your installation process include?

We check the existing shutoff, supply line and drain, replace anything worn out or the wrong size, connect the appliance, then run it and check every fitting for leaks.

How quickly can you install a new appliance?

Usually the next day. The plumbing side of a dishwasher or washer hookup takes an hour or two once we’re there, a bit longer if a valve or washer box needs replacing.

Do you serve both kitchens and laundry areas?

Yes. Dishwashers and refrigerators in the kitchen, and washing machines in the laundry room, including washer boxes, drain standpipes and the valves behind the machine.

Can you run a water line to my refrigerator for an ice maker?

Yes. If there’s no line behind the fridge, we’ll run one from the nearest cold supply and put a shutoff on it. We do this a lot, usually when someone upgrades to their first fridge with an ice maker.

Why does my washing machine drain overflow?

Usually it’s a partial clog in the drain line, or a standpipe that’s too short for how fast your washer pumps out water. We can clear the line and correct the height.
