Plumbing Fixture Installation in DFW

Swapping a faucet looks easy in a video. Then the old supply line won’t come loose, the new one doesn’t quite fit, and there’s a slow drip under the sink you don’t notice for a month. We install toilets, faucets, sinks and other kitchen and bathroom fixtures for homes in Denton, Fort Worth, Argyle, Flower Mound, North Richland Hills and nearby towns.

Fixture Installation You Won't Have to Redo

Our licensed plumbing professionals install a wide range of residential fixtures with precision and care, including kitchen and bathroom faucets, toilets, sinks, shower trim kits, laundry fixtures, and garbage disposal-compatible setups. Each installation is completed with a focus on durability, efficiency, and proper alignment, while also allowing us to identify and prevent potential issues early. Every project follows a thorough process, including safe removal of old fixtures, inspection of valves and supply lines, secure installation, proper drainage setup, and water pressure adjustments. We finish with complete leak testing to ensure reliable performance and reduce the chances of future plumbing problems.

Toilet, Faucet and Sink Installation

Toilet installation is the job we get asked about most. It sounds simple: set the bowl, bolt it down, hook up the water. But a toilet that rocks even slightly will eventually break its wax seal, and a slow leak at the base can rot the subfloor long before a stain shows up. We check the flange height, use a fresh seal, level the bowl, and test the fill valve and flush before we leave. Swapping out an older toilet?

QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Is it necessary to hire a licensed plumber for fixture installation?

For a basic faucet swap, a handy homeowner can often manage. Toilets, new sinks, shower valves, or anything that moves a drain or supply line is where a licensed plumber earns their keep. It’s the difference between a seal that holds for years and a leak you find later in the ceiling below.

What is the typical time required for fixture installation?

A faucet or toilet usually takes an hour or two. A new sink, or a shower valve behind the wall, can take most of a morning. If we find a corroded shutoff or a damaged flange once the old fixture is out, we’ll tell you before we fix it.

Do you remove existing fixtures before installing new ones?

Yes. We take out the old fixture, check what’s underneath it, and haul it away.

Can you install modern and water-efficient fixtures?

Yes. Water Sense toilets, low-flow faucets and efficient showerheads work well in most homes. The one to be careful with is a very low-flow showerhead in a house that already has weak water pressure, so we’ll check yours first.

Will my home be left clean after the installation?

Yes. We cover the floor and cabinets while we work and clean up before we go.

What happens if plumbing adjustments are needed for installation?

It’s pretty common, especially with toilets and vanities. The flange might sit at the wrong height, or a new faucet might need different supply lines. We’ll explain what’s needed and what it costs before doing anything.

Who installs new toilets in the DFW area?
