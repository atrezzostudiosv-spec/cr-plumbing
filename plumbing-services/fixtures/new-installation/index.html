03 / Plumbing

Toilet, Faucet and Sink Installation

Toilet installation is the job we get asked about most. It sounds simple: set the bowl, bolt it down, hook up the water. But a toilet that rocks even slightly will eventually break its wax seal, and a slow leak at the base can rot the subfloor long before a stain shows up. We check the flange height, use a fresh seal, level the bowl, and test the fill valve and flush before we leave. Swapping out an older toilet? Many from before the mid-90s use 3.5 gallons or more per flush. A WaterSense model uses 1.28.

Kitchen faucet installation comes up almost as often, especially pull-down and touchless models that need extra room under the sink. We make sure your sink holes, deck plate and supply lines match the new faucet before we start, so there’s no surprise halfway through. Sinks get the same treatment: mounted solidly, sealed, and tied into a drain that actually slopes the right way.

In showers and tubs, we install showerheads, trim kits and valves. With a new valve, getting temperature and pressure balanced matters more than how the handle looks. Nobody wants a shower that turns cold every time the dishwasher kicks on.

Planning a bigger bathroom remodel or a new build? We’ll handle the rough-in and drain prep, and check your water line pressure so the new fixtures work the way they should. If what you need is a repair rather than something new, our fixture repair team can help with that.

Questions / Answers

GOOD TO
KNOW.

Is it necessary to hire a licensed plumber for fixture installation?

For a basic faucet swap, a handy homeowner can often manage. Toilets, new sinks, shower valves, or anything that moves a drain or supply line is where a licensed plumber earns their keep. It’s the difference between a seal that holds for years and a leak you find later in the ceiling below.

What is the typical time required for fixture installation?

A faucet or toilet usually takes an hour or two. A new sink, or a shower valve behind the wall, can take most of a morning. If we find a corroded shutoff or a damaged flange once the old fixture is out, we’ll tell you before we fix it.

Do you remove existing fixtures before installing new ones?

Yes. We take out the old fixture, check what’s underneath it, and haul it away.

Can you install modern and water-efficient fixtures?

Yes. Water Sense toilets, low-flow faucets and efficient showerheads work well in most homes. The one to be careful with is a very low-flow showerhead in a house that already has weak water pressure, so we’ll check yours first.

Will my home be left clean after the installation?

Yes. We cover the floor and cabinets while we work and clean up before we go.

What happens if plumbing adjustments are needed for installation?

It’s pretty common, especially with toilets and vanities. The flange might sit at the wrong height, or a new faucet might need different supply lines. We’ll explain what’s needed and what it costs before doing anything.

Who installs new toilets in the DFW area?

We do. CR Plumbing, Air & Electric installs toilets all over DFW from our shops in Argyle, Flower Mound, North Richland Hills and Southlake. Call (940) 370-5663 to get one on the schedule.

Do you install restroom fixtures in commercial buildings?

Yes. We install toilets, sinks and faucets in offices, retail spaces and other commercial restrooms, and we can work around business hours when that helps.
