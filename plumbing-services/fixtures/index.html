02 / Plumbing

Plumbing Fixture Repair From a Local Plumbing Company

Most fixture problems start small. There’s a faucet that takes an extra second to shut off, a shower that runs hot then cold, or a toilet that refills by itself at 2 a.m. They’re easy to live with, but they waste water every day and usually get worse.

Hard water speeds that up here. Minerals build up on cartridges, aerators and toilet flappers until they stop sealing. A lot of the time, a fix means a new cartridge or flapper, not a whole new faucet or toilet. We’ll tell you which one you need and what each option costs.

And when it’s time to replace, we install it properly. That means new supply lines, new shutoff valves when the old ones are seized, and a leak check before we leave. Fixture work is one part of the plumbing services we provide across DFW, so if we spot a bigger issue behind the wall, you’ll hear about it.

03 / Plumbing

Bathroom Fixture Repair and Toilet Repair

Kitchen and bathroom faucets usually drip for one of three reasons: a worn cartridge, a bad O-ring or mineral buildup on the valve seat. We repair most brands. If you’re upgrading, we handle the full install, from new supply lines to the shutoff valves under the sink. Plenty of Flower Mound and Argyle homes still have their original builder-grade faucets, and a bathroom faucet installation is one of the easiest upgrades you can make.

If your shower drips after you turn it off, or the temperature jumps around mid-shower, the cartridge is the first suspect. Replacing it is usually a short job, with no tile coming off. Older or failed valves are a bigger fix, but we can often replace them from the access panel behind the shower wall. We do shower cartridge repair and shower valve replacement across Denton and Flower Mound.

Swapping a vanity sink or a kitchen sink is more than dropping in a bowl. The drain, P-trap, supply lines and disposal all have to line up and seal. We’ll install the sink and faucet together, hook up the disposal and dishwasher if there is one, and run water until we’re sure nothing drips.

Outdoor faucets take a beating in North Texas, with summer heat on one end and the occasional hard freeze on the other. A split hose bib can leak inside the wall where you’ll never see it. We repair and replace outdoor faucets, and we can install frost-free hose bibs so you’re not wrapping them every time a freeze is on the forecast.

Questions / Answers

GOOD TO
KNOW.

What plumbing fixtures do you install and repair?

Faucets, toilets, kitchen and bathroom sinks, showerheads, shower valves, tub spouts and outdoor hose bibs. If water comes out of it, we probably work on it.

How do I know if I need professional toilet services?

Call if it keeps running after a new flapper, leaks at the base, rocks when you sit on it or clogs more than once a month. A leak at the base is the one not to wait on. It can rot the subfloor.

Can you fix a clogged toilet quickly?

Usually, yes. Most clogs clear in a single visit. If it keeps coming back, the problem is likely further down the drain line, and we’ll check that too.

Do you repair shower cartridges and valves?

Yes. A dripping shower or one that swings between hot and cold usually needs a new cartridge. That’s a quick fix that doesn’t touch the tile.

Is your pricing upfront?

Yes. We give you the price before we start. If anything changes once the work is underway, we’ll stop and talk to you first.

Should I repair or replace an old fixture?

If parts are available and the body isn’t cracked or corroded, repair is usually the cheaper call. The repair-or-replace table above shows how we decide.

What areas do you serve for fixture repair?

Flower Mound, Argyle, Denton, Fort Worth and the surrounding DFW suburbs. See our service areas page for the full list.
