Whether it’s a slow drip or a full fixture replacement, we ensure your system runs reliably and efficiently again.

Repairs That Fix the Actual Problem

Plenty of fixture problems get “fixed” three or four times. Someone tightens the handle, the drip stops for a week, and then it’s back. The real cause is usually inside: a worn cartridge, a corroded valve seat, or a toilet flapper that’s warped after years of chlorinated water. So we open it up and look. Seals, cartridges, O-rings, connections, the fill valve in a toilet, the mixing valve in a shower.

QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

How do I know if I need fixture repair or replacement?

If the fixture is fairly new and one part has failed, like a cartridge, a washer or a flapper, repair almost always makes sense. If it’s old, corroded, or you’ve already fixed the same thing twice, replacing it usually costs less over time. When it’s close, we’ll give you both prices.

Do you charge by the hour or per job?

Per job. You get the price before we start, so there’s no clock running while we work.

What types of fixtures do you repair?

Faucets, sinks, toilets, showers and tubs, in kitchens, bathrooms and laundry rooms.

How fast can your licensed plumbers respond?

Often the same day or the next, depending on the schedule. If water is actively leaking, tell us when you call and we’ll move you up.

Can you fix low water pressure in a faucet?

Usually, yes. When it’s just one faucet, the cause is often a clogged aerator or cartridge, which is a quick fix. If pressure is low everywhere in the house, the problem is further back in the water line, and we’ll check that too.

Do you offer warranty on repair work?

Yes. Most of our repairs come with a service warranty, and we’ll explain what’s covered when we quote the job.

Do you serve my city?

We cover Denton, The Colony, Westlake, Argyle, Flower Mound, Fort Worth, Carrollton, North Richland Hills, Southlake and the towns around them. Not sure if you’re in range? Call and we’ll tell you.
