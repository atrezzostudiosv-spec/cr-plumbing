05 / Plumbing

Tank Water Heater Installation, Repair & Maintenance Services

At CR Plumbing, Air and Electric , we provide complete solutions for traditional hot water systems designed for long-lasting performance and reliability. Our expert team specializes in installation, maintenance, and repair, ensuring every unit is set up safely, efficiently, and according to manufacturer standards. We carefully evaluate your property’s water usage, plumbing layout, and capacity requirements before recommending the right system.

Once your system is in use, regular care becomes essential. Routine flushing, component inspection, and anode rod checks all reduce sediment buildup, improve heating efficiency, and help your tank avoid the kind of unexpected breakdown that turns into an emergency call. Homeowners who schedule maintenance consistently tend to see fewer repairs and better overall performance from their system.

If your home has a closed plumbing system — common when a backflow preventer or pressure-reducing valve is installed — your water heater also needs a properly functioning expansion tank. As water heats, it expands, and without somewhere for that extra pressure to go, it strains your tank, pipes, and fittings, often showing up as a pressure relief valve that won’t stop dripping. We install, repair, and replace expansion tanks as part of any tank water heater service call, including adding one to an existing system that doesn’t have it.

When problems do occur, our technicians provide fast, reliable repair to restore hot water — diagnosing the root cause rather than just patching the symptom, so the fix holds. For systems that need it, we also handle tankless water heater repair , so whichever type of system your home has, one call covers it.

How long does a typical tank water heater last?

A standard tank water heater typically lasts 8–12 years, depending on usage, water quality, and maintenance. Regular water heater maintenance services, such as tank flushing and anode rod inspection, can help extend the lifespan and maintain energy efficiency.

What are the signs that my tank water heater needs repair or replacement?

Common signs include inconsistent hot water, rusty or discolored water, strange noises (popping or rumbling), leaks, and rising energy bills. These issues often indicate sediment buildup or internal wear. A professional water heater repair or replacement service can diagnose the problem and recommend the best solution.

How long does it take to replace a tank water heater?

A typical tank water heater replacement takes around 2–4 hours, depending on the installation requirements. This includes removing the old unit, installing the new system, and completing all plumbing connections, venting, and safety checks to ensure code compliance.

What size water heater do I need for my home?

Choosing the right size depends on your household size, peak hot water usage, and number of bathrooms. Common residential sizes range from 30 to 80 gallons. Our team performs proper water heater sizing calculations to ensure you get reliable hot water without wasting energy.

Are tank water heaters energy efficient?

Modern energy-efficient tank water heaters are designed to meet updated federal standards, offering improved insulation and performance. While they may use more energy than tankless systems, newer models provide reliable and cost-effective hot water for most homes.

Do you offer same-day replacements if my water heater fails?

Yes, we stock common water heater sizes to provide same-day water heater replacement services whenever possible. Sudden failure can disrupt your routine, so fast service is a top priority. Our team removes the old unit, installs the new one, and completes all safety inspections and code-compliant checks—restoring your hot water quickly with minimal disruption.

Is installation by a licensed plumber really necessary?

Yes, professional installation by a licensed plumber is essential for safety and compliance. Proper gas or electric connections, venting, pressure relief valves, and drainage must meet local codes. Incorrect installation can lead to safety risks, reduced efficiency, or costly repairs.

What maintenance does a tank water heater require?

Routine water heater maintenance includes flushing the tank to remove sediment buildup, checking the anode rod, inspecting valves, and testing pressure relief systems. Annual servicing helps improve performance, extend lifespan, and prevent unexpected breakdowns.

What areas do you serve for tank water heater services?

We provide tank water heater installation, repair, and replacement services across Southlake, Argyle, Fort Worth, Denton, Flower Mound, Keller, North Richland Hills, and surrounding areas. Our local team ensures fast response times and reliable service wherever you’re located.
