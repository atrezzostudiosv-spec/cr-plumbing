QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Is trenchless sewer repair really “no-dig”?

Trenchless sewer repair is often called “no-dig” because it avoids large-scale excavation. Instead of digging up your entire yard, driveway, or landscaping, our trenchless sewer service uses only two small access points—typically near the foundation or main clean-out. This method allows us to complete a full sewer line replacement or repair with minimal disruption while preserving your property.

How long does trenchless repair take?

Most trenchless sewer repair projects are completed within a single day, depending on the length and condition of the pipe. Compared to traditional excavation methods that can take several days, trenchless solutions are faster, cleaner, and more efficient. Our professional plumbing services team ensures the process is completed quickly without compromising quality.

Is trenchless repair as durable as traditional methods?

Yes—modern trenchless sewer service methods, such as pipe lining and pipe bursting, are designed to be just as durable, if not more so, than traditional pipe replacement. The materials used are resistant to corrosion, root intrusion, and leaks, often lasting 50 years or more. This makes trenchless repair a long-term solution for homeowners looking for reliability and performance.

How do I know if I qualify for trenchless service?

Most homes with damaged or aging sewer lines qualify for trenchless sewer repair, especially if the pipe still maintains its structural path. Our team performs a video camera inspection as part of our plumbing services to assess the condition of your sewer line. If the pipe is severely collapsed or misaligned, alternative solutions may be recommended, but many common issues like cracks, leaks, and root intrusion can be resolved using trenchless methods.

What areas do you serve for trenchless repair?

We provide professional trenchless sewer service and advanced plumbing solutions throughout Argyle, Fort Worth, Flower Mound, North Richland Hills, Denton, Southlake, Irving, and nearby cities. Our team is equipped to handle residential and light commercial plumbing services with fast response times and reliable scheduling across the region.

Can trenchless repair be used on clay or cast iron pipes?

Yes, trenchless sewer repair is highly effective for older materials such as clay and cast iron pipes. These materials are prone to cracking, corrosion, and root intrusion over time. Trenchless methods allow us to restore or replace these pipes without extensive digging, improving durability and flow while extending the life of your sewer system.

Does this method come with a warranty?

Yes, all our trenchless sewer services include a workmanship warranty along with manufacturer-backed guarantees on materials. This ensures your sewer line repair or replacement is protected for the long term, giving you confidence in the durability and performance of the solution.
