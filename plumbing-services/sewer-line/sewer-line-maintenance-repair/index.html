How do I know if my sewer line needs maintenance?

Slow drains across multiple fixtures, gurgling sounds, sewage odor, or a soggy patch in the yard are the common early signs. If more than one drain in the house is slow at the same time, that usually points to the main line rather than an isolated clog.

Can you unclog a drain that keeps backing up?

Yes. A drain that clears temporarily and clogs again within weeks usually has a cause further down the line — root intrusion, buildup, or a partial break — that a standard snake won’t fully fix. We run a camera on recurring clogs to find the actual cause instead of clearing it again and sending you the same bill in a month.

Do you offer sewer line and drain service in my area?

Yes, we serve Southlake, Argyle, Flower Mound, Fort Worth, North Richland Hills, and the surrounding DFW area for both drain cleaning and full sewer line repair.

How are sewer lines repaired?

It depends on what the camera inspection finds. Minor cracks and root intrusion are often fixed with trenchless repair methods. More significant collapses or long damaged sections may need a traditional dig. We’ll walk you through which applies to your situation before any work starts.

How much do sewer line repairs cost?

The cost of sewer line repair typically ranges from $1,500 to $5,000, depending on factors such as the severity of the damage, repair method, and accessibility of the line. A professional inspection helps provide an accurate estimate based on your specific situation.

Can I finance this repair?

Yes, financing options are available for larger sewer line repairs and replacements. Ask about this when you call or during your on-site estimate.

Is this service available in Flower Mound and Fort Worth?

Yes, we provide sewer line services in Flower Mound, Fort Worth, and surrounding DFW areas. Our team serves both residential and commercial properties with reliable, timely service.

Do you handle permits?

Yes. Sewer line work frequently requires a city permit, and we manage that process as part of the job so it isn’t something you have to track down yourself.
