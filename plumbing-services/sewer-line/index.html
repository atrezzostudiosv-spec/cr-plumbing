Sewer Line Services in DFW

A sewer problem rarely announces itself politely. It shows up as a gurgling drain, a backup in the lowest tub or a soggy patch in the yard that never dries. CR Plumbing inspects, repairs and replaces sewer lines for homes across DFW.

Sewer Line Services for DFW Homes

Your sewer line is the pipe you never think about until it fails. It runs from your house to the city main or septic tank, usually buried a few feet down, and it handles everything that goes down every drain you own. When it cracks, sags or fills with roots, the whole house feels it. North Texas is rough on these pipes. Expansive clay swells and shrinks with the weather and slowly pulls joints apart.

Sewer Inspection, Repair and Replacement

Signs Your Sewer Line Has a Problem One slow drain is usually just a clog. Several at once is a different story. Watch for gurgling toilets, water backing up in the lowest tub or shower, a sewage smell inside or out, and lush green patches or soft spots in the yard along the line’s path. Foundation cracks and unexplained pest problems can also point to a broken line. If you see two or more of these, call before it turns into a backup.

QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

How do I know if I have a sewer line issue?

Look for more than one drain acting up at once, gurgling when you flush or run the washer, a backup in the lowest tub or shower, or a sewage smell. Wet or unusually green spots in the yard along the line are another sign. A camera inspection tells you for sure.

What causes sewer line damage?

Mostly tree roots, shifting clay soil and age. Older cast iron and clay pipes corrode or crack. Flushing wipes, grease and “flushable” products speeds up the buildup.

Do you offer trenchless sewer repair?

Yes, when the pipe allows it. You may see it called no-dig sewer repair. Same thing. It works best on lines that are cracked or root-damaged but still in place. If the pipe has collapsed, we’ll tell you straight that digging is the better fix. More on that on our trenchless sewer repair page.

Can you work around my landscaping?

We do our best. Trenchless repair avoids most of the yard. When we do have to dig, we plan the route to miss trees, sprinkler lines and hardscape where we can, and we’ll go over it with you first.

Do you provide a free quote?

Yes, you can request a free quote by calling or scheduling an inspection online. We typically begin with a camera inspection to assess the issue and provide an accurate estimate for your sewer line services. This ensures transparent pricing with no surprises.

Are permits required?

Often, yes. Sewer line replacement usually needs a permit and an inspection, and the rules differ by city. We handle that paperwork for jobs that require it. [PLACEHOLDER: confirm CR pulls permits]

What’s the average cost of repair?

It swings a lot. A short spot repair costs far less than replacing a long line, and depth, access and pipe material all change the number. That’s why we camera the line first and give you a written price for each option before you decide.
