North Texas water is hard, chlorinated, and rough on everything it touches — your skin, your fixtures, your water heater. CR Plumbing, Air & Electric installs whole house water filtration systems that fix it at the source, so every tap in the house runs clean. Professional Water Filtration Installation Across North Texas

Installing a water filtration system is not a fixture swap. It ties into your home’s main water line, and it has to be sized to your household’s flow rate and your water’s actual chemistry. Get either wrong and you end up with weak pressure, filters that clog in weeks, or a system that does not treat what your water actually contains. That is why we test first. Our licensed plumbers run a full water quality test at your home, hardness, chlorine, sediment, iron and total dissolved solids, and then recommend a system based on the results.

Water Filtration Installation Services

Whole House Water Filtration Installation A whole home water filtration system installs at your main water line so every fixture in the house gets filtered water. It is the right choice for most city-water homes in Flower Mound, Southlake and North Richland Hills, where chlorine and hardness are the main complaints. We size the system to your home’s flow rate so you do not lose pressure, connect it to code, and pressure-test the full system before we leave. Most whole house installations are completed in a single visit. Reverse Osmosis System Installation Reverse osmosis filters at the molecular level and is the strongest option for drinking and cooking water.

How Water Filtration Installation Works

We test your water at your home for hardness, chlorine, sediment, iron and total dissolved solids, and show you the results.

QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

How much does it cost to install a water filtration system?

Installed cost depends on the system type and your home’s size. Under-sink reverse osmosis is the lowest-cost option and treats drinking water only. Whole house filtration costs more and treats every tap. We give you the full installed price after your free water test, with labour and permits included.

How long does water filtration installation take?

Most whole house water filtration installations are finished in a single visit. Under-sink reverse osmosis systems are usually quicker. If your main line needs modification or a permit inspection is required, we will tell you upfront when we quote.

What is the difference between a water filter and a water softener?

A filter removes contaminants — chlorine, sediment, iron, bacteria. A softener removes the calcium and magnesium that cause hard water scale. They solve different problems. Many North Texas homes benefit from both, installed together at the main line.

Do I need a whole house system or just one at the kitchen sink?

If your main concern is drinking and cooking water, an under-sink reverse osmosis system may be enough. If you are dealing with scale on fixtures, dry skin, or chlorine smell in the shower, you need whole house filtration. Your water test results make this clear.

Will a water filtration system help with hard water in North Texas?

Filtration alone will not fix hardness. Hard water needs a softener. That said, most North Texas homes have both hardness and chlorine, so a combined filtration and softening system is common here. We size both together after testing your water.

Do you install reverse osmosis systems?

Yes. We install under-sink reverse osmosis systems for drinking and cooking water, including the dedicated faucet, drain connection and storage tank. RO is usually installed alongside whole house filtration rather than instead of it.

How often do water filters need to be replaced?

It depends on the system and how much sediment your water carries. Sediment pre-filters need changing most often, carbon filters less frequently, RO membranes least often. We walk you through your system’s schedule at installation and can handle replacements for you.
