Professional Water Filtration Installation Across North Texas

Installing a water filtration system is not a fixture swap. It ties into your home’s main water line, and it has to be sized to your household’s flow rate and your water’s actual chemistry. Get either wrong and you end up with weak pressure, filters that clog in weeks, or a system that does not treat what your water actually contains.

That is why we test first. Our licensed plumbers run a full water quality test at your home, hardness, chlorine, sediment, iron and total dissolved solids, and then recommend a system based on the results. Sometimes that is a whole house carbon filter. Sometimes it is a sediment pre-filter and a reverse osmosis unit at the kitchen tap. Sometimes your water is fine and you need less than you were told.

We install whole home water filtration systems, reverse osmosis systems, sediment and carbon filtration, and combination filtration and softening setups for homes across Argyle, Flower Mound, Southlake, North Richland Hills, Fort Worth, Denton and the surrounding areas. Every install includes permits, code-compliant connection to your main line, a full system test, and a walkthrough of filter replacement schedules before we leave.

Our Water Filtration Installation Services

A whole home water filtration system installs at your main water line so every fixture in the house gets filtered water. It is the right choice for most city-water homes in Flower Mound, Southlake and North Richland Hills, where chlorine and hardness are the main complaints. We size the system to your home’s flow rate so you do not lose pressure, connect it to code, and pressure-test the full system before we leave. Most whole house installations are completed in a single visit.

Reverse osmosis filters at the molecular level and is the strongest option for drinking and cooking water. We install under-sink RO systems at the kitchen tap, usually alongside a whole house filter rather than instead of one, since RO is point-of-use by design. Installation includes the faucet, drain connection, storage tank placement and a full flush and test before first use.

If your water carries visible sediment, rust or a strong chlorine taste, a sediment pre-filter and carbon filter combination usually solves it without a full treatment system. This is common on well water in the rural parts of Argyle and Denton County. We match the micron rating to what your water test actually shows.

Hard water is a separate problem from contamination, and it needs a softener rather than a filter. Many North Texas homes benefit from both, installed together at the main line. If softening is your main concern, our water softener installation page covers systems, sizing and pricing in more detail.

Filters have a service life. Once they are past it, flow drops and the system stops treating properly. We handle filter and cartridge replacement on any system, ours or one installed by someone else, and can set you up on a replacement schedule so you are not tracking it yourself.

Pressure drops, leaks at the connections, a bypass valve that has failed, a system that stopped treating. We diagnose and repair water filtration systems across all major brands, and will tell you honestly when a repair is not worth it against replacement.

Installed cost depends on three things: the type of system, your home’s size and flow rate, and what your water test shows you actually need.

An under-sink reverse osmosis system is the lowest-cost entry point and treats drinking water only. A whole house carbon filtration system costs more and treats every tap. A combination filtration and softening system sits at the top and handles both contamination and hardness together.

We give you the full installed price before any work starts, system, labour, permits and testing included. If your water test comes back clean enough that you do not need a system, we will tell you that too. Call (940) 370-5663 for a quote on your home.

Questions / Answers

GOOD TO
KNOW.

How much does it cost to install a water filtration system?

Installed cost depends on the system type and your home’s size. Under-sink reverse osmosis is the lowest-cost option and treats drinking water only. Whole house filtration costs more and treats every tap. We give you the full installed price after your free water test, with labour and permits included.

How long does water filtration installation take?

Most whole house water filtration installations are finished in a single visit. Under-sink reverse osmosis systems are usually quicker. If your main line needs modification or a permit inspection is required, we will tell you upfront when we quote.

What is the difference between a water filter and a water softener?

A filter removes contaminants — chlorine, sediment, iron, bacteria. A softener removes the calcium and magnesium that cause hard water scale. They solve different problems. Many North Texas homes benefit from both, installed together at the main line.

Do I need a whole house system or just one at the kitchen sink?

If your main concern is drinking and cooking water, an under-sink reverse osmosis system may be enough. If you are dealing with scale on fixtures, dry skin, or chlorine smell in the shower, you need whole house filtration. Your water test results make this clear.

Will a water filtration system help with hard water in North Texas?

Filtration alone will not fix hardness. Hard water needs a softener. That said, most North Texas homes have both hardness and chlorine, so a combined filtration and softening system is common here. We size both together after testing your water.

Do you install reverse osmosis systems?

Yes. We install under-sink reverse osmosis systems for drinking and cooking water, including the dedicated faucet, drain connection and storage tank. RO is usually installed alongside whole house filtration rather than instead of it.

How often do water filters need to be replaced?

It depends on the system and how much sediment your water carries. Sediment pre-filters need changing most often, carbon filters less frequently, RO membranes least often. We walk you through your system’s schedule at installation and can handle replacements for you.

Do you install systems for homes on well water?

Yes. Well water in the rural parts of Argyle and Denton County has different issues than city water – sediment, iron and bacteria rather than chlorine. We test for all of it and size the system to what your well actually produces.
