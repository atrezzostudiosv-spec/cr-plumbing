02 / Gas

OUR GAS LINE INSTALLS SERVICES

Line installs are a core part of our professional gas installation service at CR Plumbing, Air & Electric, designed to ensure safe, efficient, and code-compliant gas delivery throughout residential and commercial properties. Our licensed technicians handle complete gas line installations using approved materials and precise engineering to support long-term reliability and safety. Every project begins with a detailed evaluation of gas demand, appliance requirements, and property layout to determine the most effective routing and system design.

We provide residential solutions for appliances such as kitchens, fireplaces, dryers, and water heaters, ensuring safe and efficient operation through properly sized and installed systems like stove gas line and fireplace gas line connections. For commercial properties, we deliver scalable gas infrastructure for restaurants, offices, and retail spaces with high-demand usage requirements. Our team also manages meter-to-appliance routing, ensuring the safest pathway from the gas supply to each endpoint using durable, code-approved piping. We also specialize in system upgrades, replacements, and expansions, including water heater gas line installations, to improve capacity, performance, and compliance with current safety standards. Every installation is completed with precision, safety testing, and long-term reliability in mind.

01

Commercial Gas Installations

Our commercial gas installation services are designed for high-demand environments such as restaurants, offices, and retail facilities. We install, extend, and upgrade gas systems to support heavy gas appliance usage and continuous operation. Each system is engineered for efficiency, safety, and compliance.

02

Meter-to-Appliance Routing

We carefully plan and install gas lines from the meter to each appliance using the safest and most efficient route possible. 03

We replace outdated or damaged gas lines to improve safety, performance, and system capacity. Our upgrade services also support expansions such as adding new appliances or improving existing gas infrastructure. Every project includes leak testing and compliance verification.

We provide safe and professional gas line installation for residential properties, supporting appliances such as stoves, fireplaces, dryers, and water heaters. Our technicians design both interior and exterior routing to ensure efficient gas flow and long-term system reliability.

Frequently asked questions

GOOD TO
KNOW.

What types of gas line installations do you provide?

We install gas lines for stoves, dryers, water heaters, fireplaces, outdoor kitchens, and standby generators, for both residential and commercial properties — plus main supply lines, branch lines, and system extensions for new construction. Every installation is sized and routed for your specific appliances and property layout.

Do gas line installations require permits and inspections?

Yes, in nearly every case. Permits confirm the work meets code, and a final inspection verifies the system is safe to operate. We handle the permit filing and coordinate every inspection so you don’t have to.

How do you make sure new gas lines are safe?

Every connection is pressure- and leak-tested before it’s activated, using approved materials and code-correct fittings throughout. We also verify appliance compatibility and system pressure so nothing gets overloaded once it’s live.

How much does a gas line install cost?

It depends on line length, appliance type, property layout, and whether permitting or trenching is involved. We assess each site individually and give you a clear number before work starts — no surprise line items.

Do you replace old or undersized gas lines?

Yes. Older or undersized lines often can’t keep up with modern appliances or added household demand, which shows up as pressure problems or safety risk. We evaluate the existing system and upgrade it to current code, with pressure testing after the work is done.

How long does a gas line installation usually take?

A single appliance hookup can often be done in a few hours. A full system install or upgrade — with trenching, permitting, and inspection — takes longer. We’ll give you a realistic timeline once we’ve seen the scope.

Do you provide gas line installations for commercial properties?

Yes — restaurants, offices, retail spaces, and industrial facilities, including main supply lines, branch connections, and equipment-specific routing built for continuous, high-demand use and commercial code.

How do I schedule a gas line installation?

Start with a consultation — we look at your appliances, property layout, and what the install requires, then give you a clear scope and price. Once you approve it, we schedule the work and handle permitting, testing, and final inspection. Contact us to get started.

Who installs and repairs gas lines for stoves or outdoor grills?

CR Plumbing, Air & Electric installs and repairs gas lines for stoves, outdoor grills, and other appliances across North Texas. Our licensed technicians handle everything from a single new appliance hookup to full line replacement, all code-compliant and pressure-tested.

Which plumbing company handles gas line installation and repair in the DFW area?

CR Plumbing, Air & Electric handles both gas line installation and repair throughout the DFW area, including Argyle, Fort Worth, Flower Mound, Denton, Southlake, Lewisville, and North Richland Hills. One licensed team covers new installs, upgrades, replacements, and leak repair.
