In Texas, gas piping is a licensed trade for a reason. Residential natural gas work generally requires a licensed plumber, not a handyman. CR Plumbing Air & Electric was founded by a Master Plumber – gas is core to the trade the company was built on, not a sideline. Every line is pressure-tested and inspected. QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

What is included in your gas installation service?

A complete, end-to-end job: assessment, permits, the install or repair, pressure testing, and full safety checks. We don’t hand back a line until it’s tested and up to code.

When should I consider a new gas line installation?

When you’re adding or moving a gas appliance, building an outdoor kitchen or pool heater, remodeling, or extending gas to a new part of the house. If an existing line is old or undersized, a new run is often safer than patching.

Do you offer emergency gas leak repair services?

Yes. If you smell gas, leave the house first, then call (940) 370-5663. We respond same-day to locate and stop leaks.

What are the common signs of a home gas leak?

A rotten-egg or sulfur smell, a hissing sound near a line or appliance, an unusually high gas bill, dead vegetation near an underground line, or a pilot light that won’t stay lit. Any of these — call, don’t wait.

Do you provide gas stove repair services?

Yes — and appliance hookups generally. Ovens, ranges, dryers, water heaters, fireplaces, and generators. See gas appliances.

Can you handle gas water heater and gas heater repairs?

Yes. Gas water heaters fall under our plumbing and gas work; fireplaces have their own gas fireplace repair page.

How often should I schedule home gas repair or maintenance?

An annual safety check is reasonable for most homes, and sooner if you notice any of the leak signs above or your system is older.
