Dependable gas line solutions delivered by licensed professionals.

CR Plumbing, Air & Electric provides licensed gas line services across the Dallas–Fort Worth area: gas line installation and repair, gas leak detection, and appliance hookups. In Texas, most residential gas piping legally requires a licensed plumber — and this company was founded by a Master Plumber, so gas work is done by people qualified to sign off on it.

Gas is the one system in your house where “close enough” isn’t a category. A misread leak or a line that isn’t up to code isn’t an inconvenience — it’s a safety problem. That’s why gas work in Texas isn’t open to any handyman with a wrench: most residential gas piping requires a licensed plumber, and it’s the reason a company built by a Master Plumber has an edge here that a general contractor doesn’t.

Smell gas or suspect a leak? Leave the area immediately. From a safe location, call 911 and your gas utility's emergency number. Do not use electrical switches, open flames, or a phone near the suspected leak.

Frequently asked questions

GOOD TO
KNOW.

What is included in your gas installation service?

A complete, end-to-end job: assessment, permits, the install or repair, pressure testing, and full safety checks. We don’t hand back a line until it’s tested and up to code.

When should I consider a new gas line installation?

When you’re adding or moving a gas appliance, building an outdoor kitchen or pool heater, remodeling, or extending gas to a new part of the house. If an existing line is old or undersized, a new run is often safer than patching.

Do you offer emergency gas leak repair services?

If you suspect a gas leak, leave the area immediately and, from a safe location, call 911 and your gas utility's emergency number. Once the area is safe, contact CR to discuss any required repairs and appointment availability.

What are the common signs of a home gas leak?

A rotten-egg or sulfur smell, a hissing sound near a line or appliance, an unusually high gas bill, dead vegetation near an underground line, or a pilot light that won’t stay lit. If you suspect a leak, leave the area and call 911 and your gas utility from a safe location.

Do you provide gas stove repair services?

Yes — and appliance hookups generally. Ovens, ranges, dryers, water heaters, fireplaces, and generators. See gas appliances.

Can you handle gas water heater and gas heater repairs?

Yes. Gas water heaters fall under our plumbing and gas work; fireplaces have their own gas fireplace repair page.

How often should I schedule home gas repair or maintenance?

An annual safety check is reasonable for most homes, and sooner if you notice any of the leak signs above or your system is older.

Do I need a permit for gas line work?

Most gas line installation does require a city permit and inspection. We handle both, so you don’t have to deal with the paperwork or the delays.
