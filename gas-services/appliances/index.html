02 / Gas

OUR APPLIANCE GAS LINE INSTALLATIONS SERVICES

Appliance gas hookups are a specialized part of our professional gas line services at CR Plumbing, Air & Electric. Our licensed technicians install and connect gas lines for a variety of residential appliances, ensuring safe fuel delivery, proper ignition, and dependable long-term performance. We provide precise gas line installation for cooking appliances, water heaters, dryers, fireplaces, and outdoor kitchen equipment while following all local safety codes and manufacturer specifications.

Every appliance gas line installation begins with careful planning to confirm correct sizing, pressure regulation, and secure connection points for reliable operation. Our team performs detailed leak testing, valve inspections, and system checks to ensure every appliance functions safely and efficiently after installation. Whether you need a new stove gas line, a dedicated water heater gas line, or a professionally installed outdoor kitchen gas line, we deliver clean, code-compliant workmanship designed for lasting performance.

01

Fireplace Gas Line

We install gas line for fireplaces, gas log inserts, and decorative heating systems to improve comfort and heating efficiency throughout the home. Our technicians carefully inspect connection points, shutoff valves, and ventilation requirements to ensure safe operation.

02

Grills & Outdoor Kitchens

From standalone patio grills to fully integrated outdoor cooking spaces, we install safe and durable outdoor kitchen gas lines. Our technicians install natural gas grill hookups, shutoff valves, pressure-regulated connections, and fuel supply lines designed to withstand outdoor conditions.

03

Water Heater Gas Line

We install safe and reliable water heater gas lines to ensure consistent heating performance and efficient fuel delivery. Our technicians inspect gas pressure, shutoff valves, venting clearances, and connection integrity to support safe long-term operation.

04

Ranges and Ovens

We provide professional gas hookup for slide-in ranges, freestanding ovens, gas cookers, and other kitchen cooking appliances to ensure safe and reliable performance. Our technicians install every stove gas line with secure connections, verify proper gas pressure, and test ignition systems.

05

Dryers

We install safe, code-compliant gas lines for dryers, whether you’re switching from an electric unit or replacing an aging connection. Every hookup includes a secure shutoff valve, a pressure check, and a leak test before your dryer goes back into use.

Frequently asked questions

GOOD TO
KNOW.

Do you install gas lines for all household appliances?

Yes — stoves, ovens, dryers, water heaters, fireplaces, and outdoor cooking systems. Each installation is sized to the appliance’s gas load and your home’s existing infrastructure, with full leak and pressure testing before we call the job done.

Is a permit required for a water heater gas line installation?

In most cases, yes — new or modified gas line work typically requires a permit and inspection under local code. We handle the permitting and coordinate the inspection so the installation is fully compliant.

How do you ensure gas appliance connections are safe?

Every connection gets a pressure test and a leak test, with gas flow, shutoff valve function, and sealing checked at each point. We also confirm appliance compatibility and code compliance before and after installation.

Can you replace or modify an existing gas line for a new appliance?

Yes — resizing lines, relocating connections, or upgrading old piping to support a new or higher-demand appliance. We check your system’s existing gas capacity first, then pressure test and inspect the modification once it’s in.

What signs indicate a gas appliance connection problem?

The smell of gas, weak appliance performance, delayed ignition, a hissing sound near a connection, or visible corrosion on a fitting. Any of these warrants an inspection right away rather than waiting.

How long does a gas appliance installation usually take?

A straightforward replacement is often a couple of hours; a new line or a relocation takes longer once you factor in testing and any required permit. We’ll give you a real estimate once we’ve seen the job.

Do you work with inspectors after the installation?

Yes, whenever a permit is required. We prepare the installation for inspection — pressure testing documentation included — and handle any adjustments needed to pass.

How do I schedule a gas appliance or water heater gas line service?

Start with a consultation — we look at the appliance, the connection location, and what the job requires, then give you a clear plan and timing. Contact us to get started.

Who offers gas stove installation services?

CR Plumbing, Air & Electric offers gas stove installation across North Texas, including Argyle, Fort Worth, Flower Mound, Denton, North Richland Hills, and Southlake. Every install is code-compliant, pressure-tested, and completed by a licensed technician.

Who offers gas range installation services?

CR Plumbing, Air & Electric installs gas ranges and cooktops for homes throughout DFW, with proper sizing, secure connections, and full safety testing on every job.
