03 / Gas

Reliable Gas Repair Services for Your Home

Gas system problems should never be ignored. Even small issues involving gas appliances or fuel lines can quickly become serious safety concerns if left unresolved. Unusual odors, weak heating performance, pilot light problems, inconsistent burner operation, or rising utility bills may all indicate hidden gas system issues within the property — and a potential gas leak can create dangerous conditions around appliance connections, aging lines, valves, or damaged fittings anywhere on the property.

Many homeowners rely on professional gas stove repair when burners fail to ignite properly, flames burn unevenly, or ignition systems and gas supply components begin to malfunction. Prompt repairs restore safe, even cooking performance before the issue becomes a bigger safety concern.

Water heating issues are another common signal — inconsistent hot water, strange noises, pilot light failures, or reduced heating efficiency often point to a gas water heater repair need. Proper diagnostics catch failing components early, before they turn into a full system failure.

During colder months, homeowners also lean on us for gas heater repair — burners, ignition systems, thermostats, and gas supply connections all affect heating efficiency, and a timely repair keeps the whole system running safely through the season.

Some properties see recurring gas leak issues simply due to aging infrastructure — older fittings and outdated line materials wear down over time. Regular inspection catches this kind of gradual failure before it becomes an emergency.

At CR Plumbing, Air and Electric, we focus on delivering safe, accurate, and dependable gas repair services backed by professional workmanship and careful diagnostics — from a single appliance leak to a full property system, for homes and businesses across North Texas.

How can I tell if I have a home gas leak?

Look for a strong sulfur or “rotten egg” smell, a hissing sound near a gas line, or an unexplained jump in your gas bill. Dead vegetation near an outdoor line, appliances not working right, or symptoms like dizziness or headaches are also warning signs. If any of these show up, avoid electrical switches and open flames, and get it checked right away.

What should I do if I smell gas inside my house?

Leave the property immediately — don’t turn on lights, electronics, or anything else that could spark. Don’t try to find the leak yourself. Once you’re safely outside, call a licensed professional for emergency service.

How do you perform gas leak testing?

We use pressure testing and gas detection tools to measure concentration and pinpoint pressure drops, checking pipes, fittings, valves, and appliance connections — isolating sections of the line when needed to find even a small or hidden leak without unnecessary disruption.

Do you repair gas lines or replace them?

Both, depending on condition. Minor issues like a loose fitting or small leak are usually repairable; severely corroded or damaged lines may need full replacement. We evaluate the system first and recommend whichever actually solves the problem.

Can you repair gas leaks in appliances?

Yes — stoves, water heaters, dryers, and heating systems. We inspect the internal connections, valves, and supply lines, make the repair (or recommend replacement if that’s the better call), and test thoroughly before we’re done.

Are gas leak repairs required to pass inspection?

Yes — any identified leak has to be fully resolved before a system passes safety inspection. We make sure every repair meets local code and provide documentation when it’s needed.

How long does a gas leak repair usually take?

A small leak is often resolved in a few hours; a repair that involves line replacement takes longer. We always test and diagnose first, then confirm system integrity with a safety check after the repair.

Do you provide gas leak repair for both homes and businesses?

Yes — from a single home appliance leak to a larger commercial gas system issue, using the same detection tools and safety standards across every property type.

Which company handles both water and gas leak detection in DFW?

CR Plumbing, Air & Electric handles both water and gas leak detection across DFW, using certified testing equipment for either system. One licensed team can diagnose and repair both, rather than requiring two separate calls.

Who do I call for a gas leak in my house?

If you smell gas or suspect a leak, leave the property and call a licensed gas repair company immediately — CR Plumbing, Air & Electric provides emergency gas leak detection and repair across Argyle, Fort Worth, Flower Mound, Denton, Southlake, and the surrounding DFW area.
