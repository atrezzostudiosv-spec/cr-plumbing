02 / Gas

Why Choose Us for Gas Fireplace Repair

A lot of people aren’t sure who to call about a gas fireplace. A chimney sweep? An HVAC company? The gas company? For most problems, it’s a plumber. The fireplace runs on a gas line, a shutoff, a valve and a burner, and that’s gas work we do every day.

What we find is usually pretty ordinary. Dust, and sometimes spider webs, clogging the burner ports and pilot tube after a summer off. A thermocouple that’s worn out and can’t hold the valve open. A valve that sticks. Now and then, a loose fitting on the gas line.

None of that means replacing the fireplace. It means finding the one part that’s failing and fixing it before it turns into a safety problem.

03 / Gas

Gas Fireplace Problems We Fix

The pilot won’t stay lit. This is the call we get most. The pilot lights, you let go of the knob, and it goes out. Usually it’s the thermocouple or thermopile, the small sensor that tells the valve the pilot is burning. They wear out over time, and replacing one is a quick job.

The fireplace won’t turn on at all. Check the easy things first. Is the gas valve at the wall open? Does the remote or wall switch have fresh batteries? If both check out, the problem is usually the igniter, the valve, or a pilot tube plugged with dust.

The flame is small, uneven or yellow. Burner ports clog over time, which throws off the flame pattern and makes the fireplace burn dirty. A tall, yellow flame with soot on the glass or logs isn’t just cosmetic. It means the gas isn’t burning cleanly, and it’s worth having someone look at it.

Something’s wrong with the gas line. In older homes, or after a remodel, the line feeding the fireplace can leak at a fitting, or the flex connector may be old enough to need replacing. We repair and replace fireplace gas lines and pressure test them before the gas goes back on.

Most of this is preventable. A yearly check handles a lot of it. Fall is the right time, before you actually need the fireplace. We clean the burner and pilot, check the valve and connections, and make sure it lights and runs the way it should.

Frequently asked questions

GOOD TO
KNOW.

How do I know if I need gas fireplace repair?

If the pilot won’t stay lit, the fireplace won’t turn on, the flame looks small or yellow, there’s soot on the glass, or you hear clicking with no flame, it’s time to have it checked. Smelling gas is different. Turn it off, get out of the house, and call your gas company.

Do you provide gas fireplace maintenance service?

Yes. A yearly service, ideally in the fall, covers cleaning the burner and pilot, checking the valve, testing ignition and looking over the gas connections.

Can you repair damaged fireplace gas lines?

Yes. We’re licensed plumbers, so we repair and replace the gas line and connector that feed the fireplace, then pressure test everything before the gas goes back on.

What does a gas fireplace technician inspect during service?

The pilot assembly, the thermocouple or thermopile, the igniter, the burner, the gas valve, the connector and shutoff, and how the flame actually looks while it’s running. On vented units we also check that the vent is clear.

Do you handle gas fireplace valve replacement?

Yes. If the valve sticks or doesn’t control the flame properly anymore, we’ll replace it with one that matches your fireplace.

Is gas fireplace burner repair included in your services?

It is. A clogged burner is one of the most common reasons a fireplace burns poorly, and cleaning or repairing it is a regular part of what we do.

How often should I schedule a gas fireplace tune up?

Once a year. Fall is best, before the first cold snap and before everyone else calls at the same time.

Do you offer gas fireplace repair in my area?

We cover Argyle, Fort Worth, Flower Mound, Lantana, Denton, Southlake and the towns nearby across North Texas.

Who do I call to fix a gas fireplace?

For most repairs, a licensed plumber. Pilot, valve, burner and gas line problems are all gas work. If you smell gas, call your gas utility first. If it’s a wood-burning fireplace that needs its chimney cleaned, that’s a job for a chimney sweep.
