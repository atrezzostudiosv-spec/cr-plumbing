How do I know if my gas line needs replacement or just a repair?

If you smell sulfur, see corroded piping, or have inconsistent appliance flames, schedule a pressure test; minor leaks can be patched, but widespread corrosion usually means a full line replacement.

Can you install a standby generator and connect it to my existing gas supply?

Yes. We size the generator to your load, run a dedicated gas branch line, obtain permits, and perform a leak-down test to ensure code compliance and safe operation.

What is trenchless sewer repair and will it damage my yard?

Trenchless methods reline or burst damaged sewer pipes through small access points, saving landscaping and driveways while restoring full flow integrity.

What warranties come with your services?

Most repairs include a one-year labor warranty; new equipment carries 5- to 10-year manufacturer coverage.

Is hydro-jetting safe for old cast-iron pipes?

Yes—when set to the correct pressure. Our techs adjust PSI to scour debris without harming pipe walls, then camera-inspect to confirm cleanliness.

How do I improve indoor air quality without replacing my entire HVAC system?

Add MERV-13 filters, UV lights, or a bypass air purifier. These retrofit into existing ductwork and reduce pollen, dust, and airborne pathogens.

Why does my dryer vent need professional cleaning?

Lint buildup restricts airflow, triples drying time, and is a leading cause of house fires. An annual vacuum and brush service restores efficiency and safety.

Are ductless mini-splits efficient for whole-home cooling?

Yes. Multi-zone mini-splits can cool or heat every room, offer SEER ratings above 20, and allow independent temperature control per zone.

When should I choose a dehumidifier versus a humidifier?

Use a dehumidifier when indoor relative humidity exceeds 60 %, common in Texas summers. A humidifier helps in winter when RH drops below 30 %, preventing dry skin and static.

How often should my AC and furnace be serviced?

Schedule maintenance twice a year—AC tune-up in spring, furnace check in fall. Regular service maintains warranties, lowers energy bills, and prevents costly breakdowns.
