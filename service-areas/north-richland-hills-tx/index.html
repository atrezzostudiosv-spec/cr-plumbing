A lot of houses in North Richland Hills went up in the ’60s, ’70s and ’80s. That means original cast iron drains, galvanized supply lines, and electrical panels that weren’t built for today’s appliances. Add the clay soil that shifts every summer and you get slab leaks and cracked sewer lines that show up out of nowhere.

Our techs see this stuff all the time. They’ll tell you what’s actually wrong, what can wait, and what can’t. No upselling a new water heater when a $40 part fixes it.

Need a plumber in North Richland Hills on short notice? We keep trucks stocked so most repairs get done on the first visit.

Plumbing Repair in North Richland Hills

Drain cleaning is the big one. Kitchen lines clog with grease, older cast iron mains build up scale, and tree roots find their way into clay sewer pipe. We snake or hydro jet the line, then run a camera through it so you can see the cause yourself.

When the camera shows a cracked or bellied sewer line, we’ll walk you through repair options, including trenchless sewer repair that saves the yard. We also handle water line repair, burst pipes, slab leak detection and full repiping for homes still running on galvanized.

Water heaters get their own trip list. Tank or tankless, gas or electric, we install, repair and replace them. A heater that’s 10 to 12 years old and leaking from the tank usually isn’t worth fixing. One that just needs a thermocouple or element usually is. We’ll tell you which.

Heating & HVAC Service

North Richland Hills winters are short but they still bite, and a furnace that hasn’t run since February loves to quit on the first cold night. Our techs repair gas furnaces, electric heaters and heat pumps, and we check the heat exchanger and flue while we’re there because a crack there is a carbon monoxide risk.

In summer it flips. AC repair, refrigerant leaks, dead capacitors and systems that run all day without cooling the house. If yours is old enough that repairs keep stacking up, we’ll price out a replacement, but we won’t push one.

Electrical Work

Breakers that keep tripping, outlets that feel warm, lights that flicker when the AC kicks on. These aren’t things to ignore in an older home. Our electricians troubleshoot the cause, repair wiring, upgrade panels and add circuits for things like EV chargers and new kitchens.

Gas Line Installation & Repair

Smell gas? Leave the house and call us from outside. We find gas leaks, repair the line and pressure test it before the gas goes back on. We also run new gas lines for ranges, dryers, fireplaces, outdoor kitchens and generators.

Water Filtration & Softening

NRH water is treated and safe, but it’s hard. That shows up as white crust on faucets, spotty dishes and water heaters that fill with sediment. A whole-home softener or filtration system helps with all three, and we’ll test your water first so you’re not buying more system than you need.

Ask the team ↗
Do you offer plumbing services in North Richland Hills?

Yes. We’re one of the busiest plumbers in North Richland Hills, handling everything from a dripping faucet to a full sewer line replacement. Drain cleaning, water heaters and leak repair are our most common calls here.

Are your technicians licensed?

Yes. Our plumbers, electricians and HVAC techs are licensed in Texas and background checked. You’ll get the tech’s name before they arrive.

What plumbing services do you offer in North Richland Hills?

Drain cleaning, sewer line repair, water heater installation and repair (tank and tankless), water line repair, slab leak detection, repiping, gas line work, sump pumps, fixtures and garbage disposals.

Do you offer emergency plumbing in North Richland Hills?

We do. Burst pipe, sewage backing up, no hot water, gas smell. Call and we’ll get a tech out as fast as we can. If it’s a burst pipe, shut off the main valve while you wait. It’s usually at the curb or where the line enters the house.

Can you help with gas line installations and repairs?

Yes. We repair gas leaks, run new lines for appliances and outdoor kitchens, and pressure test everything before the gas is turned back on.

Do you install water filtration systems?

Yes. We install whole-home filtration, softeners and under-sink reverse osmosis. We test your water first so you know what you’re actually treating.

Do you repair furnaces and heaters in North Richland Hills?

Yes. Gas furnaces, electric heat and heat pumps. If your heater is blowing cold air, short cycling or making a burning smell that doesn’t go away after the first run, give us a call.

Do you serve Hurst, Watauga and Richland Hills too?

Yes. Along with North Richland Hills, we cover Hurst, Watauga, Richland Hills, Haltom City, Keller, Colleyville and the rest of the Fort Worth area.

How much does a plumber cost in North Richland Hills?

It depends on the job. A clogged drain and a sewer replacement aren’t in the same ballpark. What we can promise is a written price before work starts, so the number on the bill is the number you agreed to.
