03 / Electrical

ALL WHOLE-HOME SURGE PROTECTION SERVICES WE OFFER

Unexpected power surges can damage HVAC systems, appliances, and sensitive electronics in an instant. Whether it’s from utility-side switching, a lightning strike nearby, or the power coming back on after an outage, a whole-house surge protector installed by CR Plumbing, Air & Electric stops the spike before it reaches your equipment.

Modern homes rely on more electronics than ever — smart thermostats, entertainment systems, appliances, and HVAC controls all share the same panel. A single unprotected surge event can damage several of them at once. Installing a whole-home surge protector reduces that risk across the board, not just for whatever happens to be plugged into a strip.

Outdoor electrical systems are just as vulnerable. A properly installed surge protector helps guard pool equipment, landscape lighting, and outdoor outlets against the same spikes that threaten what’s inside.

Lightning and severe weather are the most dramatic cause, but far from the only one. Voltage fluctuations from grid switching or nearby high-power equipment cycling on and off cause smaller, repeated stress on your electronics — the kind of wear that shortens a system’s life without ever tripping a breaker.

Whole-home protection also improves the safety and lifespan of major appliances and HVAC equipment. Repeated small surges shorten the life of expensive systems even when no single event causes visible damage — preventive protection reduces that wear over time.

For added defense, outdoor and detached spaces — garages, workshops, entertainment areas — can be protected with dedicated point-of-use devices layered on top of the main panel protection.

Many homeowners also benefit from pairing surge protection with a professional electrical panel inspection to catch aging wiring or an undersized panel before it becomes its own problem.

Frequently asked questions

GOOD TO
KNOW.

Which electrical company installs whole-house surge protectors in the DFW, TX metro?

CR Plumbing, Air & Electric installs whole-house surge protection across Fort Worth, Argyle, Flower Mound, Keller, North Richland Hills, Southlake, and the surrounding DFW area — licensed electricians, installed at the panel, tested before we leave.

Why do I need whole-home surge protection?

A power strip only protects what’s plugged into it. Whole-home protection guards everything on your electrical panel at once — HVAC, appliances, and wiring — from a single point of failure.

Does surge protection stop lightning damage?

It significantly reduces the risk from lightning-related spikes reaching your home’s electrical system, though a direct strike can still cause damage no surge protector fully prevents. It’s risk reduction, not a guarantee.

What types of electronics does surge protection help protect?

HVAC systems, major appliances, home electronics, smart home devices, and anything connected to your electrical panel — not just what’s plugged into an outlet.

Who installs panel upgrades with surge protection included?

We do — surge protection is commonly installed alongside a panel upgrade, since both involve the same panel work. Ask us to assess both at the same visit.

Do I still need power strip surge protectors if I install whole-home protection?

Whole-home protection is your first line of defense at the panel. Point-of-use strips add a second layer for especially sensitive electronics, but aren’t required once whole-home protection is in place.

How long does it take to install whole-home surge protection?

Most installations are completed same-day.

Does whole-home surge protection require maintenance?

Very little — we test and verify the system at install, and it’s worth a check during any electrical inspection. It’s not a device you need to service regularly.

Can a surge protector prevent damage to HVAC systems?

Yes — HVAC systems are among the more expensive equipment a surge can damage. Panel-level protection covers the system along with everything else on the circuit.

Does my home need surge protection if I rarely have outages?

Yes. Surges aren’t the same as outages — many happen from grid switching or nearby equipment with no visible power loss at all. Rare outages don’t mean rare surges.
