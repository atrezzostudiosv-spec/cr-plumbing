whole-home surge protection Services

Every appliance, HVAC system, and piece of electronics in your home shares the same electrical panel — a single surge can hit all of them at once. We install panel-level surge protection sized to your home’s actual electrical load, positioned to catch spikes before they reach anything downstream. We also install point-of-use protection for the equipment that needs it most — HVAC systems, refrigerators, and home electronics — layered with the panel-level device for complete coverage rather than relying on one line of defense. Every installation includes testing, monitoring compatibility, and a code-compliance check, so the system isn’t just installed — it’s verified to actually work. This is also part of our electrical inspection services, if you’d like your whole panel reviewed at the same time.

All Whole-Home Surge Protection Services We Offer

Unexpected power surges can damage HVAC systems, appliances, and sensitive electronics in an instant. Whether it’s from utility-side switching, a lightning strike nearby, or the power coming back on after an outage, a whole-house surge protector installed by CR Plumbing, Air & Electric stops the spike before it reaches your equipment. Modern homes rely on more electronics than ever — smart thermostats, entertainment systems, appliances, and HVAC controls all share the same panel. A single unprotected surge event can damage several of them at once. Installing a whole-home surge protector reduces that risk across the board, not just for whatever happens to be plugged into a strip.

All Whole-Home Surge Protection Services We Offer

Panel-Level Surge Protection Advanced panel-level surge protection installation providing comprehensive defense against voltage spikes and lightning-induced electrical surges throughout your entire electrical system. Professional installation includes proper grounding connections, indicator monitoring, and equipment selection ensuring maximum protection effectiveness for electronics, appliances, and smart home technology while maintaining manufacturer warranties on protected devices. Point-of-Use Surge Protection Strategic point-of-use surge protection installation for sensitive electronics including computers, entertainment systems, and medical equipment requiring additional protection beyond whole-home systems.

QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Which electrical company installs whole-house surge protectors in the DFW, TX metro?

CR Plumbing, Air & Electric installs whole-house surge protection across Fort Worth, Argyle, Flower Mound, Keller, North Richland Hills, Southlake, and the surrounding DFW area — licensed electricians, installed at the panel, tested before we leave.

Why do I need whole-home surge protection?

A power strip only protects what’s plugged into it. Whole-home protection guards everything on your electrical panel at once — HVAC, appliances, and wiring — from a single point of failure.

Does surge protection stop lightning damage?

It significantly reduces the risk from lightning-related spikes reaching your home’s electrical system, though a direct strike can still cause damage no surge protector fully prevents. It’s risk reduction, not a guarantee.

What types of electronics does surge protection help protect?

HVAC systems, major appliances, home electronics, smart home devices, and anything connected to your electrical panel — not just what’s plugged into an outlet.

Who installs panel upgrades with surge protection included?

We do — surge protection is commonly installed alongside a panel upgrade, since both involve the same panel work. Ask us to assess both at the same visit.

Do I still need power strip surge protectors if I install whole-home protection?

Whole-home protection is your first line of defense at the panel. Point-of-use strips add a second layer for especially sensitive electronics, but aren’t required once whole-home protection is in place.

How long does it take to install whole-home surge protection?

Most installations are completed same-day.
