Questions / Answers

GOOD TO
KNOW.

What is a standby generator and how does it work?

A standby generator system is a permanently installed backup power solution that automatically turns on when the main electrical supply goes out. It continuously monitors your home’s power supply and activates within seconds of an outage, restoring electricity to essential systems like HVAC, refrigeration, lighting, and medical devices without manual intervention.

How do I know what size generator my home needs?

The right home generator sizing depends on your total electrical load, including essential appliances, HVAC systems, and critical circuits. Our electricians perform a detailed load assessment to recommend the correct standby generator capacity, ensuring your system can safely handle your home’s backup power needs without overload.

What fuel types are available for standby generators?

Most standby generators run on natural gas or propane. Natural gas is a popular option due to its continuous supply from utility lines, while propane is ideal for homes without gas access. Both fuel types offer reliable, long-duration backup power solutions during outages.

Do standby generators require maintenance?

Yes, regular generator maintenance services are essential to ensure reliability. This includes oil and filter changes, system inspections, battery checks, and load testing. Routine servicing helps your backup generator system perform efficiently and reduces the risk of failure during emergencies.

How long can a standby generator run during an outage?

A properly fueled standby generator system can run for extended periods, often days or even weeks, as long as it has a continuous fuel supply like natural gas or sufficient propane storage. This makes it a dependable emergency power solution during long-term outages.

Is a permit required for backup generator installation?

Yes, most areas require a permit for standby generator installation services. This ensures the system meets local electrical and safety codes. Our team handles all necessary permits and inspections, making the installation process smooth, compliant, and stress-free for homeowners.

Can I use a portable generator instead of a standby generator?

Yes, a portable generator can be used as a temporary backup power source. However, it requires manual setup, proper transfer switch installation, and careful operation to avoid safety risks like backfeed. While useful in emergencies, a standby generator system offers automatic, hands-free protection.

How long does installation take?

The timeline for a generator installation service depends on system size, electrical setup, and permitting requirements. Most installations take a few days once permits are approved, including site preparation, wiring, fuel connection, and system testing to ensure full operational readiness.
