Frequently asked questions

GOOD TO
KNOW.

What types of electrical installations do you offer?

We provide a full range of residential electrical installation services, including lighting installation, ceiling fans, outlets and switches, dedicated circuit installations, appliance wiring, EV charger installation, and complete home electrical system upgrades. Whether it’s a small upgrade or a full rewiring project, our licensed electricians ensure safe and reliable results.

When should I upgrade my electrical panel?

You should consider an electrical panel upgrade if your home has frequent breaker trips, flickering lights, or if you’re adding high-power appliances like HVAC systems, EV chargers, or new kitchen equipment. Older panels may not handle modern energy demands safely or efficiently, making an upgrade essential for performance and safety.

Do electrical upgrades increase home value?

Yes, modern electrical upgrades can significantly increase your home’s value. Buyers often look for updated electrical systems, energy efficiency, and code compliance. Upgrades like new panels, smart wiring, and energy-efficient lighting make your home more attractive and reduce future maintenance concerns.

What should I expect during an installation appointment?

During your electrical installation service appointment, our technician will first assess your current system, discuss your needs, and provide a clear plan. Once approved, we complete the installation safely, test all components thoroughly, and ensure everything is working according to code. We also clean up the workspace before leaving.

How long do electrical installations typically take?

The duration of an electrical installation project depends on the complexity of the job. Simple installations like switches or lighting may take a few hours, while larger projects such as panel upgrades or rewiring can take a full day or more. We always provide a clear timeline before starting work.

Are your installations compliant with local and national electrical codes?

Yes, all our electrical installation and repair services are fully compliant with local regulations and the National Electrical Code (NEC). Safety is our top priority, and we ensure every project meets strict industry standards for reliability and long-term performance.

Do you install smart home electrical systems?

Yes, we offer smart home electrical installation services, including smart lighting, automated switches, smart thermostats, and integrated home control systems. These upgrades improve convenience, energy efficiency, and overall home automation.

How do I know if my home needs electrical upgrades?

Common signs that you may need electrical system upgrades include frequent breaker trips, outdated wiring, limited outlets, dimming lights, or reliance on extension cords. If your home is older or you’re adding new appliances, a professional inspection can help determine if an upgrade is needed for safety and efficiency.
