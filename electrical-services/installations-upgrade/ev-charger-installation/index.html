ABOUT THE SERVICE

CR Plumbing, Air & Electric installs Level 2 EV chargers across the Dallas–Fort Worth area — indoor or outdoor, on any major vehicle brand and connector standard. Every install starts with a panel load assessment, includes proper GFCI protection and grounding, and is completed by a licensed electrician to code.

A standard wall outlet charges an EV slowly enough that it barely matters — a Level 2 charger is the difference between “topped up overnight” and “still not full in the morning.” Getting there safely means checking whether your panel can actually handle the added load before anything gets installed, not after. That’s the assessment-first approach here, whether the charger is going in the garage or out on the driveway.

As more homeowners switch to electric vehicles, a reliable home charging setup becomes essential. Our licensed electricians specialize in installing EV chargers that meet safety codes, match your vehicle’s requirements and integrate seamlessly with your electrical system. Whether you’re upgrading your garage or preparing for your first EV, our professional electrical services ensure a smooth, efficient installation across Argyle, Fort Worth, Flower Mound, North Richland Hills, Denton and Southlake.

03 / Electrical

EV Charger Installation Services for Convenient & Reliable Home Charging

Not every home is already set up for high-power EV charging. Before installing anything, we assess your panel’s existing capacity, wiring condition, and available load — and tell you honestly whether it can support a Level 2 charger as-is or needs an upgrade first. Skipping this step is how homes end up with overloaded circuits and unreliable charging.

We install chargers compatible with all major EV brands and both connector standards in common use — Tesla’s connector (including Wall Connector installations) and the standard J1772 connector used by most other manufacturers. Whether you’re set on a specific brand like ChargePoint or want a recommendation based on your vehicle, we handle the install and the compatibility.

Every EV charger installation we complete includes GFCI protection and proper grounding — required safety measures, not optional extras. Skipping either is how you end up with an overloaded circuit, unstable power flow, or a long-term electrical problem that damages both the charger and your home’s wiring.

A lot of EV charger installs aren’t in a finished garage — they’re on a driveway, under a carport, or in a detached building. We install weatherproof enclosures and durable outdoor mounting so the charger holds up outside, not just a garage-rated unit stretched somewhere it wasn’t built for.

Most Level 2 chargers we install support app connectivity — scheduling charging for off-peak electricity rates, monitoring energy use, and remote start/stop from your phone. We set this up as part of the install, not as a separate visit.

EV charging technology and household energy needs both keep moving — more vehicles, higher power demands, and for some homeowners, pairing the charger with a home solar setup. We install with that trajectory in mind, so your system isn’t obsolete the moment your needs change or you add solar.

What’s the difference between Level 1 and Level 2 EV chargers?

A Level 1 EV charger uses a standard 120V outlet and provides slower charging, typically adding 3–5 miles of range per hour. A Level 2 EV charger installation uses a 240V dedicated circuit and delivers much faster charging, often 20–40 miles of range per hour. Most homeowners choose EV Level 2 charger installation for convenience, efficiency, and daily usability.

Do I need an electrician to install a Level 2 charger?

Yes, professional installation is required for a safe and code-compliant setup. A licensed electrician handles wiring, breaker installation, and proper grounding during your EV charger installation. This ensures your home EV charger installation operates safely and meets all local electrical standards.

Will my electrical panel support an EV charger?

It depends on your current electrical capacity. Before any electric vehicle charging station installation, we perform a load calculation to determine if your panel can handle the added demand. If needed, we recommend an electrical panel upgrade to safely support your EV charger and prevent overload issues.

How long does EV charger installation take?

Most home EV charger installation projects are completed within a few hours to one day. The timeline depends on factors like panel capacity, wiring distance, and installation complexity. Our goal is to complete your EV charger installation service quickly while ensuring safety and quality.

Do I need permits for installing an EV charger?

In most areas, permits are required for EV charger installation, especially for Level 2 systems. We handle the permitting process as part of our electrical installation services, ensuring your setup meets all local codes and inspection requirements.

Can you install chargers for all EV brands?

Yes, we install chargers for all major brands, including Tesla, ChargePoint, and other universal systems. Our electric vehicle charging station installation services are compatible with most EV models, ensuring flexibility and long-term usability.

Will installing a charger increase my electricity bill?

Charging your EV will increase electricity usage, but it is typically more cost-effective than fuel. A properly installed home EV charger improves charging efficiency, and many homeowners take advantage of off-peak rates to reduce costs. Our EV charger installation services help optimize energy use for better savings.

Is my EV charger installation future-proof?

Yes, when installed correctly, your system can support future EV upgrades. We design every EV Level 2 charger installation with scalability in mind, ensuring your electrical system can handle increased demand or additional chargers. This makes your home EV charger installation a long-term investment in convenience and sustainability.
