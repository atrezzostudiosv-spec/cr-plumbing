QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

How do I know if my electrical panel needs to be upgraded?

Common signs include frequent breaker trips, flickering lights, burning smells, buzzing sounds, or reliance on extension cords due to limited outlets. If your home still uses a fuse box or an outdated panel, it may not support modern power demands. A professional electrical panel upgrade through certified residential electrical services ensures your system can safely handle today’s appliances and prevents overload risks.

What size electrical panel do most homes need today?

Most modern homes require a 200-amp electrical panel, though smaller homes may use 100–150 amps depending on usage. Larger homes or those with EV chargers, HVAC systems, or heavy appliances may need higher capacity. During an electrical panel upgrade, electricians assess load requirements to recommend the correct size for safe and efficient power distribution.

What happens during a panel upgrade?

A typical electrical panel upgrade involves removing the old panel, installing a new electrical panel, upgrading breakers, and reconnecting circuits. Electricians may also perform circuit breaker replacement, grounding improvements, and system labeling. All work is completed with strict code compliance and safety checks.

How long does a panel upgrade take?

Most electrical panel upgrade projects take between 4 to 8 hours, depending on system complexity, wiring condition, and whether additional upgrades (like breaker panel replacement or rewiring) are needed. Power is usually temporarily shut off during installation.

Will upgrading my panel increase my home’s value?

Yes, a modern electric panel upgrade can increase property value by improving safety, reliability, and capacity. Buyers prefer homes with updated electrical systems, making this upgrade a strong investment as part of overall home electrical services.

Can I install a new electrical panel myself?

No, installing or replacing an electrical panel is not a DIY task. It requires licensed professionals due to safety risks, permits, and code requirements. Hiring experts for electrical installation services ensures proper installation, inspection approval, and long-term system safety.

Does an electrical panel upgrade reduce energy bills?

An electrical panel upgrade doesn’t directly lower energy consumption, but it improves efficiency and stability. It allows better distribution of power, supports energy-efficient appliances, and prevents system strain—often reducing indirect costs related to electrical issues and repairs.
