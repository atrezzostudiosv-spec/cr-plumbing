Questions / Answers

GOOD TO
KNOW.

What do residential electrical services typically include?

Our residential electrical services cover everything needed to keep your home safe, functional, and up to code. This includes home electrical repair and installation services, panel upgrades, lighting installations, inspections, and advanced solutions like EV charger installation and home battery backup. Whether it’s a small fix or a full system upgrade, services are tailored to meet your home’s current and future power needs.

When should I consider an electrical panel upgrade?

If your home has an outdated panel, struggles to handle modern appliances, or you’re planning additions like an EV charger or new HVAC systems, it may be time for an electric panel upgrade or a new electrical panel. Upgrading ensures your system can safely manage increased electrical loads and reduces the risk of overloads or outages.

Do you provide electrical installation services for new homes or renovations?

Yes, our electrical installation services include new construction wiring, system expansions, lighting upgrades, and electrical outlet installation. We ensure all installations meet safety standards, are properly load-balanced, and designed for long-term efficiency.

What are the benefits of installing an EV home charger?

An EV home charger installation allows you to charge your electric vehicle safely and conveniently at home. Compared to standard outlets, a dedicated charger provides faster charging, improved safety, and better energy management. It also prepares your home for the growing shift toward electric mobility.

What backup power solutions are available for homes?

We offer a range of backup power solutions, including generators and advanced whole home battery backup systems. A home battery backup stores energy for use during outages, keeping essential systems like lighting, refrigeration, and medical devices running. These systems are ideal for homeowners who want uninterrupted power and greater energy independence.

Why is whole house surge protector installation important?

A whole house surge protector installation protects your electrical system and appliances from sudden voltage spikes caused by lightning, grid issues, or internal surges. Without proper protection, these surges can damage expensive electronics and reduce the lifespan of your devices.

What types of lighting services do you offer?

We provide both indoor lighting installation and LED lighting installation to improve energy efficiency and enhance your home’s ambiance. Whether you’re upgrading outdated fixtures or designing a new lighting layout, modern lighting solutions can significantly reduce energy consumption while improving visibility and comfort.

Do you offer emergency electrician services?

Yes, we provide fast and reliable emergency electrician service for urgent issues like power outages, exposed wiring, or electrical failures. Immediate professional attention helps prevent safety risks and restores your system as quickly as possible.

Which areas do you provide residential electrical services in?

We proudly offer our full range of residential electrical services across Argyle, Flower Mound, North Richland Hills, and nearby communities.
