Electrical problems rarely announce themselves clearly. A warm outlet, a buzzing switch, a breaker that trips on Tuesdays but not Mondays. The hard part is finding what’s behind it, and that’s where experience matters more than tools. Our electricians work on everything from 1960s homes with original wiring to new builds with full smart panels. Same approach either way.

Usually it’s too much load on one circuit, a loose connection, or a failing device somewhere down the line. If it’s an AFCI or GFCI breaker, it’s often sensing damaged wire or moisture.

QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

What qualifies as a general electrical repair in my home?

Anything that involves fixing existing wiring, devices or protection. Dead outlets, bad switches, tripping breakers, damaged wire, loose connections, failing light fixtures and panel repairs all fall under it. New installs and upgrades are a separate category.

When should I call for general electrical services instead of waiting?

Call right away for burning smells, scorch marks, warm outlets or cover plates, sparking, a hot panel, or repeated breaker trips on the same circuit. Those point to heat somewhere in the system. Flickering lights and the occasional dead outlet can wait a day or two, but not a month.

Why does my breaker keep tripping more than it used to?

Usually one of three things. You’ve added load to that circuit, a connection has worked loose, or a device on the circuit is failing. An older breaker can also weaken and trip at less than its rating. Testing the circuit under load tells us which it is.

What are the signs my home may have outdated wiring?

Two-prong outlets with no ground, a fuse box instead of breakers, cloth or rubber-sheathed wire visible in the attic or basement, no GFCI protection in kitchens and baths, and a 60 or 100-amp service in a house running modern appliances. Any one of these is worth an inspection.

Is underground wiring harder to repair than indoor wiring?

It takes more work to reach, yes. Finding the fault means tracing the line and then digging at the right spot. The repair itself is straightforward once it’s exposed. We use direct-burial rated wire and conduit so the fix holds up.

What causes a sudden electrical outage in a single room?

Most often a tripped GFCI outlet somewhere else on the circuit, like a garage or bathroom, since one GFCI can protect several rooms. After that, it’s usually a loose connection in an outlet or switch box. Check your GFCI outlets and press reset before you call.

How often should electrical maintenance be scheduled?

Once every few years for most homes, and yearly for homes over 40 years old or businesses with heavy equipment. Also worth doing when you buy a house, after a remodel, or after storm or flood damage.
