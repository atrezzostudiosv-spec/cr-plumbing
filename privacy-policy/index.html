Privacy notice

Your request details

This website presentation is managed by Atrezzo Studios. The request forms let you enter, review and copy your service details in your browser. Online delivery is not active during design review. No request or appointment is submitted by these forms.

Information you enter

The forms ask for your name, phone number, city, requested service, an optional email address, a message and, where applicable, a preferred date. These details are used to generate a local preview of your request. Please avoid entering payment details or other sensitive information.

Copying and contacting the team

The copy button writes your request details to your device clipboard. If you choose to send an email or call the team separately, the information you share will be handled through that service. Contact atrezzostudiosv@gmail.com with questions about this website presentation.

Other website services

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and some photographs from the original CR website. External links to Google Maps, Google reviews, YouTube or other services are subject to their own policies.
