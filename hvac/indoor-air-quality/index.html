KNOW.

What exactly does an indoor air quality system do?

An indoor air quality system improves the air inside your home by removing pollutants such as dust, allergens, bacteria, and airborne particles. As part of a professional indoor air quality service, these systems use solutions like an air purifier for home, high-efficiency air filters for home, UV lights, and air scrubbers to create cleaner, healthier air. Installed by a trusted indoor air quality company, these systems work seamlessly with your HVAC setup to continuously filter and treat the air circulating throughout your home.

How do I know if my home needs an air purification system?

Signs you may need an air purification system include frequent allergies, dust buildup, musty odors, uneven airflow, or consistently poor indoor air quality. A professional indoor air quality service can identify these issues and recommend solutions such as an air purifier for home or upgraded air filters for home. Homes with pets, smokers, or high humidity levels especially benefit from these systems, as they help improve air cleanliness, balance airflow, and create a healthier, more comfortable living environment.

What is the difference between MERV 13 and HEPA filtration?

MERV 13 filters are high-efficiency HVAC filters that capture smaller particles like dust, pollen, and mold spores while still allowing proper airflow in most home systems. HEPA filtration systems provide even higher filtration, trapping up to 99.97% of microscopic particles, including bacteria and fine pollutants. However, HEPA systems often require specialized equipment or modifications to your HVAC system.

Are UV lights and ionizers safe for my home?

Yes, professionally installed UV air purification systems and ionizers are safe when used correctly. UV lights help neutralize bacteria, viruses, and mold within your HVAC system, while ionizers assist in reducing airborne particles. Our team ensures all indoor air quality installations meet safety standards and are properly configured for your home.

How long does indoor air quality installation take?

Most indoor air quality services can be completed within a few hours, depending on the type of system and your existing HVAC setup. Whether it’s installing air filters, UV lights, or whole-home air purification units, our technicians ensure a quick and efficient installation with minimal disruption.

Will an indoor air quality system reduce allergy symptoms?

Yes, improving indoor air quality can significantly help reduce allergy symptoms by removing common triggers like dust, pollen, pet dander, and mold spores. High-performance air filtration and purification systems create a cleaner environment, making it easier to breathe and improving overall comfort.

How often do indoor air quality systems need maintenance?

Regular indoor air quality maintenance is essential for optimal performance. Filters should typically be replaced every 1–3 months, while UV lights and other components may need annual inspection or replacement. Routine servicing ensures your air purification system continues to operate efficiently and effectively.

Can indoor air quality systems help reduce odors in my home?

Yes, indoor air quality systems can help eliminate odors caused by cooking, pets, smoke, and humidity. Advanced air purification and filtration systems work to neutralize odor-causing particles, leaving your home fresher and more comfortable.
