Dryer vent installation, repair and cleaning for DFW homes

A dryer pushes hot, damp air and lint out of the house through a duct that most people never look at. When that duct gets crushed behind the machine, packed with lint, or runs too far with too many bends, the dryer works harder, clothes stay damp, and lint builds up where it shouldn’t. That’s a fire risk, and it also costs you money every time you run a load. CR Plumbing, Air & Electric works on all of it. As a dryer vent contractor, we handle dryer vent repair and installation, cleaning out a clogged line, and fixing a loose elbow or a disconnected joint. If you aren’t sure what you need, call and describe what the dryer is doing. We’ll point you in the right direction.

Dryer vent care & warning signs

What to know about vent installation, cleaning, repairs and the signs of restricted airflow.

01

Installation, cleaning & repairs

Here’s what we do, when each service makes sense, and where to go for more detail.

New home, new dryer, or a vent that’s been wrong since the day it went in? We run rigid or semi-rigid metal duct on the shortest, straightest path to an outside cap, secure the joints, and keep the bends to a minimum. If your old vent is foil, vinyl or crushed behind the machine, replacing it is usually the real fix. Learn more about dryer vent installation .

Lint collects in the duct and at the cap, and the screen inside the dryer catches only part of it. Cleaning the full length of the run restores airflow and removes fuel from a place where heat is present. We recommend it once a year for most households, more often if you dry a lot of laundry or have pets. See dryer vent cleaning and repair .

Most damage happens at the connections: a loose elbow behind the dryer, a split joint in the attic, a dented duct squashed by a washer. We fix or replace the damaged section, seal it properly and check that air moves freely when we’re done. This is also where dryer exhaust repair comes in when the vent has come apart or the cap is stuck shut.

Questions / Answers

GOOD TO
KNOW.

Who does dryer vent installation near me?

CR Plumbing, Air & Electric does, across the DFW area. Our technicians run the duct, set the outside cap and test airflow before they leave. Call us and we’ll tell you what your home needs.

What makes a dryer vent installation safe and up to code?

Rigid or semi-rigid metal duct, a short and mostly straight run, tight joints that aren’t held together with screws that catch lint, and an outside cap with a working flap. The exact limits depend on your dryer’s manual and local code.

How do I know if my dryer vent is clogged?

The classic signs are clothes that take longer than one cycle, a hot dryer or hot laundry room, and a burning or musty smell. Check the outside cap, too. If it doesn’t open or is full of lint, the duct likely needs attention.

How often should I have my dryer vent cleaned?

Once a year works for most homes. A big family, lots of laundry, pets, or a long vent run with several bends means it’s worth doing more often.

Can a dryer vent repair or replacement be done in one visit?

Usually, yes. Cleanings and most repairs are one visit. Moving the vent to a new wall or fixing a long run in an attic can take longer. We’ll tell you before work starts.

When should I schedule a professional dryer vent inspection?

When the dryer is slow, when you move into a new home, when you replace the dryer, or when it’s been a year or more since the last cleaning. An inspection is a good idea any time you’re unsure what’s behind the machine.
