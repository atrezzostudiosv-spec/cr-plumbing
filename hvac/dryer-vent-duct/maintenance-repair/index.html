Dryer duct repair and dryer vent repair , done properly

A dryer is basically a hot-air machine, and all that damp, warm air has to go somewhere. It travels through a duct and out of the house. When that duct is crushed behind the dryer, packed with lint, pulled apart at a joint, or full of bends, the dryer takes longer to dry clothes, runs hotter, and drops lint where it doesn’t belong. CR Plumbing, Air & Electric repairs clothes dryer vents for homeowners across DFW. We clear lint, fix broken elbows and joints, reconnect or replace damaged sections, and check that the whole run works when we’re done. Not sure where the problem is? Describe what the dryer is doing. We’ll work it out.

Here’s what we do on a repair visit and how to tell which service you need. (Give the H3s ids for the jump links: lint-removal, elbow-joint-repair, dryer-vent-cleaning.)

We start by looking at the dryer, the connection behind it, the duct run to the outside, and the cap. We check for lint, crushed or kinked sections, loose or separated joints, and a cap that won’t open. Then we explain what we found and what it would take to fix. You approve the price before we start working.

Lint builds up in the duct and at the cap, and the filter inside the dryer only catches some of it. We clear the duct along its full length and clean the cap, then check that air is moving freely. If a vent is clogged enough that clothes won’t dry, this is often the whole fix. We also handle clogged dryer vent repair when lint has packed so tightly that it takes more than a quick clean.

Most dryer exhaust problems happen at the connections. An elbow behind the dryer gets crushed when the machine is pushed back. A joint in the attic splits. A section pulls loose from the wall. We repair or replace the damaged part, secure it, and seal it so lint and moist air don’t end up inside the wall.

Once a year is a sensible schedule for most homes. If you do a lot of laundry, have pets, or your duct has a long run or several bends, you may want to do it more often. We offer dryer vent cleaning across DFW, including Fort Worth, Dallas and Flower Mound. [Confirm the cities CR serves for this service.]

Repair makes sense when the problem is a clog or a single damaged section. Replacement is the better option if the whole duct is foil or vinyl, crushed in several places, too long, or in poor shape. We’ll tell you plainly which one applies and give you both prices when it’s close. If you need a new vent, see our dryer vent installation (/hvac/dryer-vent-duct/new-installation/) page.

Questions / Answers

GOOD TO
KNOW.

Who does dryer duct repair near me?

CR Plumbing, Air & Electric repairs dryer ducts and vents across the DFW area. Call us, tell us what the dryer is doing, and we’ll book a visit.

How do I know if my dryer vent needs repair or cleaning?

The signs are clothes that need more than one cycle, a dryer or laundry room that feels hot, a burning or musty smell, and lint or a stuck flap at the outside cap. A technician can confirm whether it’s a simple clog or a damaged duct.

How often should I schedule dryer vent maintenance?

Once a year is a good rule for most homes. More laundry, pets, or a long, bendy duct can mean more often

What does a professional dryer vent cleaning include?

We clear lint from the duct and the outside cap, check the connections for damage, and make sure the air flows freely when we’re done.

Do you repair damaged elbows, joints and duct sections?

Yes. We repair or replace crushed elbows, loose joints and damaged sections, secure them properly and seal the connections. If most of the duct is in bad shape, we’ll talk to you about replacing it instead.

What materials do you use for dryer vent repairs?

Rigid or semi-rigid metal duct, with proper clamps or tape at the joints. We avoid flexible foil or vinyl duct in places where it can sag, crush or trap lint.
