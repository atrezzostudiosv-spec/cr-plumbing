Dryer vent installation that sends the air outside

Every dryer pushes a lot of hot, wet air and lint toward the outside of your house. The duct is the only thing between that and your walls, attic or crawlspace. When it’s the wrong material, too long, or got squashed when someone slid the dryer back, it quits doing its job. CR Plumbing, Air & Electric installs and replaces dryer vents for homeowners across DFW. A new build, a new laundry room, or a duct that’s been wrong for years. We handle all of it, and you deal with one dryer vent contractor from the first call to the last test.

What goes into an installation, and how to tell if you need one. (Give the H3s ids for the jump links: new-vent-installation, duct-replacement, vent-rerouting.)

A new dryer needs a way out. We find the best exit, which could be the wall behind the dryer, the roof or the rim joist, and plan the duct to go there in the fewest turns we can manage. Then we run the duct, fasten it, seal the joints and fit the outside cap. Once it’s in, we run the dryer and make sure the air leaves the house freely.

Plenty of homes still have the original duct, and it’s often foil or vinyl. Those materials sag, tear and hold onto lint. If your duct is old, crushed behind the dryer, patched together or much longer than it should be, replacing it is usually better than patching. We remove the old duct and put in a metal one that fits the space. If yours only needs a cleaning or a small fix, we’ll say so. See dryer vent repair and cleaning (/hvac/dryer-vent-duct/maintenance-repair/).

Some vents are the way they are because of where the dryer ended up. A long run through the attic, or a vent that makes three turns before it reaches daylight. Moving the exit point or rerouting the duct can cut the length and the bends. Shorter runs dry faster and collect less lint. Whether it’s possible depends on the house, so we look at it before we promise anything.

Rigid metal is the best choice for most of the run. In tight spots, a short length of semi-rigid metal helps get around a corner. We stay away from foil and vinyl flex because they sag, tear and trap lint. Check your dryer’s manual and local code for limits on length and number of bends.

• The dryer takes more than one cycle to dry a load.

• The duct is white vinyl, foil, or looks crushed or kinked.

• The outside cap has a stuck flap or is covered in lint.

• The run to the outside is long and has several bends.

• You’ve just bought a new dryer or moved the laundry room.

You get a trained technician from our own team. We handle plumbing, heating, cooling and electrical, so if the dryer needs a gas line or a new outlet, we can look at that too.

Questions / Answers

KNOW.

Who does dryer vent installation near me?

CR Plumbing, Air & Electric installs dryer vents across the DFW area. Call us, tell us about your setup, and we’ll set up a visit.

What type of material is best for dryer duct installation?

Rigid metal for most of the run, and semi-rigid metal where you need to bend around something. We avoid vinyl and foil flex duct because it sags, tears and traps lint.

How do you decide the size and route of the duct?

We look at where the dryer sits, where the nearest outside wall or roof is, and what’s in the way. Then we plan the shortest path with the fewest turns. The duct diameter follows your dryer’s manual.

Yes. Dryer exhaust has rules about duct material, length, bends and where it can end. The details depend on your dryer and your city.

Yes, all dryer vent installations must comply with local building codes and fire safety regulations. This includes using approved materials, proper vent termination, and maintaining safe duct lengths. Our installations follow industry standards, ensuring your system is both safe and fully compliant.

How long does dryer vent installation take?

A simple installation is often done in a few hours. A longer reroute through an attic or wall can take longer. We’ll give you a time estimate after we’ve seen the setup.

Can a new dryer vent improve how well my dryer works?

Often, yes. A clear, short duct lets the dryer push air out easily, so clothes tend to dry faster and the dryer doesn’t have to run as long. We can’t promise a number. It depends on what was wrong with the old vent.

When should I replace or install a dryer vent?

When you get a new dryer, build a laundry room, find foil or vinyl duct, notice crushed or kinked sections, or see lint around the cap. If the dryer is slow and a cleaning doesn’t fix it, a new duct is probably next.
