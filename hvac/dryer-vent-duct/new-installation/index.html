Dryer Vent Installation in DFW

A bad dryer vent is easy to live with until it isn’t. Longer dry times, a hot laundry room, lint in the walls. If yours is crushed, too long or made of foil, we’ll replace it with a proper duct and run it the shortest way out.

Dryer vent installation that sends the air outside

Every dryer pushes a lot of hot, wet air and lint toward the outside of your house. The duct is the only thing between that and your walls, attic or crawlspace. When it’s the wrong material, too long, or got squashed when someone slid the dryer back, it quits doing its job. CR Plumbing, Air & Electric installs and replaces dryer vents for homeowners across DFW. A new build, a new laundry room, or a duct that’s been wrong for years.

QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Who does dryer vent installation near me?

CR Plumbing, Air & Electric installs dryer vents across the DFW area. Call us, tell us about your setup, and we’ll set up a visit.

What type of material is best for dryer duct installation?

Rigid metal for most of the run, and semi-rigid metal where you need to bend around something. We avoid vinyl and foil flex duct because it sags, tears and traps lint.

How do you decide the size and route of the duct?

We look at where the dryer sits, where the nearest outside wall or roof is, and what’s in the way. Then we plan the shortest path with the fewest turns. The duct diameter follows your dryer’s manual.

How long does dryer vent installation take?

A simple installation is often done in a few hours. A longer reroute through an attic or wall can take longer. We’ll give you a time estimate after we’ve seen the setup.

Can a new dryer vent improve how well my dryer works?

Often, yes. A clear, short duct lets the dryer push air out easily, so clothes tend to dry faster and the dryer doesn’t have to run as long. We can’t promise a number. It depends on what was wrong with the old vent.

When should I replace or install a dryer vent?

When you get a new dryer, build a laundry room, find foil or vinyl duct, notice crushed or kinked sections, or see lint around the cap. If the dryer is slow and a cleaning doesn’t fix it, a new duct is probably next.
