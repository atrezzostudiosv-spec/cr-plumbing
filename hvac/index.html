QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

How do I know if AC repair or replacement is smarter?

Start with age and history. A system under 10 years old with one failed part is usually worth repairing. One that’s 12 to 15 years old and has needed repairs two summers running is usually not. If it uses R-22 refrigerant, a major repair rarely pays off. The repair-or-replace table above lays out how we decide.

Are warranties included?

New equipment comes with the manufacturer’s warranty, and our installation work carries a workmanship warranty.

Do seasonal maintenance plans matter?

They do, mostly because they get the check done. A spring AC tune-up and a fall heating check catch small problems, like a weak capacitor or a dirty burner, before they turn into a no-cool or no-heat call.

How can I improve airflow in my home?

Start with the easy things: change the filter, make sure registers and returns aren’t blocked by furniture and keep the outdoor unit clear. If airflow is still weak, the cause is often leaky or undersized ducts, and a tech can test for that.

What causes uneven temperatures in different rooms?

Usually it’s ductwork. Leaks, kinked runs, undersized ducts or a weak return can all starve a room. Poor attic insulation and two-story layouts make it worse. A duct and airflow check shows which one is behind it.

Do smart thermostats really make a difference?

A little. They help most with scheduling, so you’re not cooling an empty house all day. They won’t fix a system that’s undersized or leaking air. Pair one with a system that’s running well and you’ll notice it on the bill.

My dryer vent seems clogged — is that urgent?

It’s worth doing soon. Lint is flammable, and a restricted vent traps heat. If your dryer takes more than one cycle to dry a load, or the laundry room gets hot, get the vent cleaned.
